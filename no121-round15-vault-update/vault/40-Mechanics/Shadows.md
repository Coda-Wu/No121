# Shadows

Ratified on 13 and 14 November 2026 through the shadow feel test. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]], [[Purge and Light]] and [[Sanity]].

Combat stays small and frightening because the camera never improves. Each new shadow breaks the last lesson, and no shadow is worth hunting.

## Seven rules, no upgrade tree

1. **The kit is fixed.** Six frames on waking, ten at most, three hits, and a resident's food as the only cure.
2. **One frame has three uses.** It files an anomaly, repairs a distortion, or keeps a shadow. Every shadow kept is a report not filed.
3. **Only the back can be taken.** A shadow that is looking at him smears the print and lunges.
4. **The finder makes him weak.** With the camera raised he walks at a third of his pace and sees only the frame.
5. **Nothing drops.** A kept shadow leaves one print, and the print shows what it was imitating.
6. **Most shadows are his own doing.** A floor has one or two authored shadows. The rest hatch from sick anomalies that he missed, two alive at most. See [[Festering]].
7. **Home goes quiet, and the frontier does not.** A purged room stays safe.

## The lesser shadow

- **It is missing light.** It is drawn in the light layer, as ink, and it moves smoothly, like ink in water. It does not sit on the pixel grid.
- **Its facing reads from its lean.** Its head leads. It has no face.
- **Stillness is the tell.** When it notices him it stops swaying and draws itself up.
- **The flash carves it** along the cone. Carved below half, it lets him pass.
- **It mends**, facing where he stood when the flash went off. The mend looks like ink merging, and like shadows joining by destructive interference.
- **It ends in one of three ways.** He leaves it. He keeps it with a frame from behind, which costs film and gives a print. Or it is gone in a holding light, which costs no film and gives no print.

## The Sleeper

- It lies as a mound under a dead lamp. Walking past it or over it does nothing.
- The flash wakes it, if the beam or its spill reaches it. His sleep wakes it too, on a bench or in a Dreamwalk.
- Awake, it hunts whoever is awake. A flash only pauses it.
- It has no back, so no frame keeps it. It is passed, and not beaten.
- **A bench stops it.** If he sits and shuts his eyes, it walks to him, stands over him, pours into the floor and sleeps beside the bench. It stays there.

## Both

- Neither takes a stair or the lift. Leaving the corridor always works.
- Neither crosses smoke.
- **The Suit cannot see a shadow.** The Suit can still be hit, and cannot flash or file. See [[Persona Law]].

## Hits

Three hits, shown as smoke on his own outline. The third closes the picture like an eye, and he comes to in [[Room 202]] as the Suit. He loses place and time, and nothing else. See [[Sanity]].
