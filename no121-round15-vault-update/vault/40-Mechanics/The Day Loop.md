# The Day Loop

> [!note] Revised in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. The five beats and certification are retired. The rules below them stand.

Day = chapter = episode.

**Saving is beat-level and invisible** — see [[Saving]]. The old rule, *autosave at wake*, was written for 21–35 minute chapters and is retired; wake points survive only as the chapter-select surface.

## What a day is now

- **Its shape as story** is in [[Episode Structure]]: a cold open, a request, a complication, a break, an act-out.
- **Its shape as play** is in [[The Walk]]: a floor built as a lock, a gate he cannot pass, a resident and a maze, and a verb that comes home with him.
- **Its tasks** come from [[Requests]], [[The Pocket Note]] and the place itself.

## Diagnosis and the four verbs

These stand.

- **Diagnosis** — lawful obeys a clause; sick violates one you know. See [[The Two-Grammar Rule]].
- **Resolution verb** — **EXPLOIT** (use a clause to steer or traverse) / **PURGE** (light or photo, see [[Purge and Light]]) / **CAPTURE** (evidence, see [[The Camera]]) / **ESCAPE** (a pocket).

## Reference channels (anti-pixel-hunt)

Yesterday's prints (the board is haze-proof memory) · the House Rules · [[Otto]]'s childhood memory as terse examine lines · plain domestic sense.

## Day-advance rules
- **Only the day's critical beat advances the day.** A day may end the moment that beat lands, wherever he is standing.
- Scripted blackouts only, 3–5 game-wide, film-cut style; [[Eve]] puts you to bed.
- **Timers are permitted where a mechanic needs one.** Revised in Round 12; the old rule was *zero timers anywhere*, and it is retired. There is still no timer on a day, a chapter, an exploration or a conversation — nothing that pressures the player to stop thinking. Timers exist only **inside** a mechanic that is about elapsed time.
  - Settled example: the **afterimage decay** is timer-based. See [[Locks and Openers]].
- **In-world clocks still lie.**
- 🔧 Idle sleeps used to escalate the dream, one new detail for each fruitless night. With no nightly loop the hint system needs a new carrier. See [[The Dream and Hint Ladder]].

## Day math

**Revised in Round 8.** The target is **25–40 minutes per chapter**, across **8 cards** — the cold open, six days, and the final night. The cold open runs short and [[07 DEED — The Family]] runs long and varies by player, by design.

That lands the game at **4–5 hours**, raised from 3–4 h in [[Commercial Frame]].

🔧 The demo was set at 35–50 minutes for the cold open plus D1. Round 15 adds the opening dream, about ten minutes of memory rooms and about ten minutes of the Queen game. The figure needs a new measurement.
