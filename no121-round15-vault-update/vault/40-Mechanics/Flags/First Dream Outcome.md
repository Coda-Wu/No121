# First Dream Outcome

## What the Building remembers

How [[Otto]] left the opening dream: **by the lit exit**, or **by dying in it**, which is ten hits after the lessons are given.

A restart from a checkpoint does not set it. Only the way the dream ends does.

## Surfaces where it shows
- [[07 DEED — The Family]] — for a player who died in the first dream, some chases are made easy on purpose. When the player senses that something is wrong, [[Mr. Vacancy]] asks in narration whether this difficulty suits him.
- It does **not** touch the bridge. The bridge decides on its own what the camera survives.
