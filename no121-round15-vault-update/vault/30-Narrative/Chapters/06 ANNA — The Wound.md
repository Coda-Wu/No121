## Beats
- **The flooded fifth floor.** Otto works in the water. This is the floor where the drowning lives, and it is the rehearsal for the descent into the heart.
- **The lung maze.** At the end of each of its acts, a first-person view plays, from Otto's or from [[Anna]]'s eyes. This is her side in this chapter. See [[Her Side]].
- Otto keeps **the level** from this chapter. See [[Abilities and Gates]].
- The dig.
- The Building grows [[Anna's Flat]] — a room on no plan, furnished from [[Otto]]'s memories.
- **The darkroom, second playing.** Her undeveloped roll is found and developed on [[Renner]]'s rig. It is the second and last time the player develops by hand. Key item: **her love** — the one photograph among the eight. See [[Key Items]] and [[The Camera]].
- **The evening call.** It opens with the same sentence [[Anna]] used at 3 AM on D1, then she says something only a living person would say, in the future tense, and Otto does not correct her. Cut with flashbacks to the day she went missing.
- [[Reverse Film]] window opens.
- **Chapter ending — the rubbing of the eyes.** The player is put back on the beach on 8 August 2002 and experiences the loss directly: the wind, the sand, the hands going to the eyes, the empty water afterwards. See [[The Delusions]].
