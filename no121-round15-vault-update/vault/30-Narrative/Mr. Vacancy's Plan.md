# Mr. Vacancy's Plan

> [!warning] Top-secret layer. Ratified in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]], [[Mr. Vacancy]] and [[The Delusions]].

## The plan

[[Mr. Vacancy]] is using [[Otto]]'s journey. It feeds him hope and then breaks it, so that his sense grows, and it lets every resident help him. When he is strong enough it means to consume both him and [[Anna]], and to grow by it.

It formed the plan in [[The Opening Dream]]. A burst of flash burned its copies, and the one true raven saw what Otto could become.

## One trap with two mouths

The vault already says that the demon wants Otto to give up first. The two plans fit together.

- **If Otto accepts an account,** he gives up, and it takes Anna.
- **If Otto refuses,** the refusal costs him and sharpens his sense. A sharper Otto is worth more to it.

The delusions are therefore built to be survived. The player believes that resisting is winning.

## The loop inside each episode

1. **Hope.** Something suggests that she can be reached.
2. **Reach.** Otto acts on it, usually with a resident's help.
3. **Break.** A delusion turns that hope against him.
4. **Gain.** He comes out of it seeing more than before.
5. **Use.** The gain is his tool in the next episode, and the demon's next lever.

## What it permits

- **The Agent sees shadows because it lets him.** See [[Persona Law]].
- **The residents' help is real, and it is also what the demon counts on.** Each side request makes Otto stronger for the last run and richer as prey. See [[Requests]].
- **It cannot enter [[Room 202]].** Nothing reaches the board except through Otto. See [[The Corkboard Thread]].

## How the player learns it

**No character explains the plan.** The player feels it, and most sharply in the bad endings, where the Otto they sharpened is what is taken. See [[Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach]].

For that to work the pattern must be legible in play. A gain follows every break. The player should be able to notice it by the third time. One moment of suspicion is allowed before [[07 DEED — The Family]], in [[05 REPAPER — The Twins]].

In DEED it speaks once in its own voice. For a player who died in the opening dream, some chases are made easy, and it asks whether this difficulty suits him. See [[First Dream Outcome]].

## How it fails

The sense that it grew in Otto is what lets him see the Queen's shadow armour and strip it. The plan produces the one weapon that defeats it. See [[Her Side]]. This agrees with the standing gate: only an intact camera reaches [[I See You — The True Ending]].
