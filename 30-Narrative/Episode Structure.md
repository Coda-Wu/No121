# Episode Structure

Ratified in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. It replaces the narrative half of [[The Day Loop]] and all of [[Night Phase]].

**A card is an episode.** The calendar is kept: [[02 SPOOL — The Job]] is still Day 1 and [[07 DEED — The Family]] is still Day 6. The seams between days are no longer played. The player never walks [[Otto]] to a desk to receive a task, and never walks him home to end a day.

## The parts

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Cold open** | The first image is inside the plot. The player has control within seconds. |
| **Title card** | The palindrome, after the cold open. |
| **Act 1, the request** | Someone or something asks. See [[Requests]] and [[The Pocket Note]]. |
| **Act 2, the complication** | The space answers back: a maze, a shadow, an anomaly. See [[The Walk]]. |
| **Act 3, the break** | [[Mr. Vacancy]] turns the episode's hope against him. See [[Mr. Vacancy's Plan]]. |
| **Act-out** | The episode ends on the beat that changes what Otto believes. It cuts. He does not walk home. |
| **Tag** | A tape, a call or a beat of her side. Only where it carries plot. See [[Tapes to Anna]] and [[Her Side]]. |

## Rules for an opening
1. No two episodes open in the same place.
2. The player never travels in order to begin.
3. The opening image belongs to the thread that leads the episode. See [[The Threads]].
4. Only [[02 SPOOL — The Job]] opens on a full morning in [[Room 202]]. A routine has to exist once before breaking it can mean anything.

## Rules for an ending
1. The last beat is the object won or the hope broken.
2. It may happen anywhere in the Building, or outside it.
3. Nothing playable follows it.
