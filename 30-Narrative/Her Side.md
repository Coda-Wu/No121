# Her Side

Ratified in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. It is one of the five [[The Threads|threads]].

[[Anna]] does not know that she is dead, and [[Otto]] never says that the Building is her. Her side of the story is what the player learns of her from her own end.

## One reveal for each chapter

**Her side is shown once in every chapter, near its end, and in a different form each time.** It is not always the Queen game.

| Form | Where |
|---|---|
| A telephone call | The 3 AM ring in [[02 SPOOL — The Job]]. The evening call in [[06 ANNA — The Wound]]. |
| A first-person view, from his eyes or from hers | At the end of each act of the lung maze in [[06 ANNA — The Wound]]. |
| The Queen game | The demo, and the last run. See below. |
| 🔧 Other forms | [[03 LIVED — The Name]], [[04 RADAR — Detection]], [[05 REPAPER — The Twins]], [[07 DEED — The Family]]. |

## The Queen game

The game on the PC in [[Room 202]]. Alice runs from the Queen of Hearts. Alice is Anna. The Queen is [[Mr. Vacancy]]. Otto is the Hatter. He may feel her in it. He never says so.

- **His first try is on his first night, and he loses.** It is a memory room. It must come before any key item. See [[The Memory Rooms]].
- **In the demo** the player reaches the first defeat of the Queen's guard. The survivor play in the demo takes about ten minutes.
- **It must change form.** A player cannot be asked to play the same survivor game seven times or more. After one play as a survivor game it shifts, as *Inscryption* does. One named form: a short top-down adventure in pixel art, with a simple map and one objective, in which the player controls Alice. She finds one secret about where she is, or speaks to one person in that wonderland, or plays one beat.
- **Key items help her.** What Otto wins in the Building becomes a help to Alice. Help flows from him to her.

## The last run

On the final night the burning of the usher is played as the Queen's last run. See [[08 REDIVIDER — The Door]].

- Otto enters her game as a companion.
- His camera strips the Queen's shadow armour, and Alice can then defeat her.
- A cracked camera cannot strip it. This is the same gate as the bridge in [[07 DEED — The Family]].
- Residents whose side requests were completed help in the run. See [[Side Requests Completed]].
- **They are forced apart when the fight ends.** [[Eve]] said that visiting hours end at three. The run ends at 3:00.

## Superseded

The earlier cadence of three story runs, with optional runs on every other night and pages of her escape that fall back to an idle screen, is superseded in part by the change of form. 🔧 The redesign of the Queen game is not yet written. The desk beside the PC, where a key item changes the game's idle screen, is not affected.
