# The Tomb Reading

> [!warning] The authored false conclusion. It must be fully derivable, internally consistent, and wrong. See [[The Ontology]] for the truth it hides.

## The claim the player is meant to reach

*№121 is Anna's grave. She is buried in it, the residents are mourners or remains, and the Building exists to keep her body.*

Everything in that sentence is supportable from evidence the player can find, photograph, develop and pin. The correction — that it is a **lifeboat**, built by her to keep herself, not by anyone to keep her — arrives only at the endings.

## Why the misdirection is honest

The tomb reading is not a lie told to the player; it is the reading the evidence best supports until the last day. A grave and a lifeboat look identical from inside when the occupant is the architecture. The reveal does not overturn the evidence — it re-reads it.

Under the twist test in `35-Corkboard/PROTOCOL.md`, the correction recontextualises every planted item below rather than contradicting any of them.
