# The Body

> [!warning] Top-secret layer. See [[The Ontology]] and [[The Death of Anna]].

## Where she is

[[Anna]] drowned at sea in 1998 ([[Timeline]]). Her body was never recovered. It lies in deep water, wrapped in **adipocere** — body wax, which forms in cold, oxygen-poor conditions and holds a body's shape for decades.

She is **decaying slowly, not gone**. The rate of decay is the rate at which the Building fails.

## How the player sees it

Through **dimensional distortion** inside the Building. The corpse is visible in-game without [[Otto]] ever leaving to retrieve it — he does not go and get her.

Directly, once: in the heart, at the base of the lightwell, at the end of [[09 REDIVIDER — The Door]].

## How the body is restored

[[Reverse Film]] plus **all** of the mission-reward key items — see [[Key Items]]. Reverse Film alone produces a body with nothing in it: [[Do I Know You — The Hollow Return]].

## Consequences already in canon

- Each floor corresponds to an organ; anomalies on that floor are generated from that organ. See [[The Organ Map]].
- The lung is the wound, because she drowned. [[The Sealed Fifth Floor]] is flooded.
- Body-horror anomalies across the whole Building originate here, in the decaying body. The other anomaly source is the death wish — see [[The Two-Grammar Rule]].
