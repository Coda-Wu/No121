# The Curse

> [!warning] Top-secret layer. See [[The Ontology]] and [[Mr. Vacancy]].

## What it is

[[Mr. Vacancy]] **existed before [[Anna]] died.** It is a demon, and it took her family one at a time. The family was cursed; **Anna was the last one it wanted.**

Its method is not force. It creates a **mismatch in information** — a gap between what a person perceives and what is happening — and uses the gap to take a life and to leave the survivors with an account they cannot reconcile.

## Anna's death, as the curse

Otto was watching Anna swim. Wind blew sand into his eyes; he rubbed them; when he could see again, **Anna and everyone else on the beach were gone.**

The wind was the demon's. The blindness was the demon's. The mismatch it opened — Anna saw a man on the beach who did not come, Otto saw an empty stretch of water — is what it has been using to delude them both ever since.

## The two readings

- **The natural reading**, which is what any inquest would find: a rip current, and the instinctive drowning response — a drowning person cannot call out or wave, stays upright and silent for twenty to sixty seconds, and does not look like a person in trouble.
- **The true reading:** the demon made both of those things happen, and made Otto blind for the length of them.

Both accounts are complete. Neither can be checked from inside the Building.
