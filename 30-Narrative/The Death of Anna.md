# The Death of Anna

> [!warning] Top-secret layer, and the spine the rest of the fiction hangs from. See [[The Ontology]].

> [!note] 🔧 This note is deliberately a `seed`. Round 1 settled that she died; Round 2 settles how, why, and what she wants. Nothing here is canon until Coda answers.

## What is settled

- [[Anna]] went missing and died in **1998** — see [[Timeline]].
- She is genuinely dead. Not sleeping, not sealed in a room, not rescuable by being found.
- What survives is a fading fragment of consciousness, and that fragment is the Building.
- **[[Eve]] tells the truth about the death**, in the clinical register, and the player is set up to disbelieve her. She is the only character who never lies about it.
- Keeping the Building intact keeps the fragment alive, which keeps open the chance of bringing her back.
- She chose [[Otto]] and called him, five years late.

## The open spine

These four questions are load-bearing and unanswered. Each one changes the endings.

1. **How did she die?** The manner of death sets what the vessel is compensating for, and therefore what every sick anomaly is made of.
2. **Why did she die?** Whether it was done to her, chosen by her, or neither, decides whether the game has a culprit outside the Building.
3. **Why did the call take five years?** Working hypothesis: a consciousness needs time to take shape, and it took that long to grow into something that could hold a building and reach outward. The hypothesis does not yet explain the harder version of the question —
4. **Why was Otto not reached first?** He was her partner. He should have been the first voice she found, and he was the last. Any answer that does not hurt is the wrong answer.

Coda's Round 1 note: *"all people who believed Anna still lives are now dead, so maybe naturally Otto becomes the next one to be called"* — recorded as a candidate, not adopted.

## Does she want to live?

Unresolved, and the most important unresolved thing in the game. The answer decides whether [[True Ending — Informed Breach]] is a rescue or an imposition, and whether [[Neutral Ending — Stay]] is a surrender or a kindness.

If the fragment wants to live, the True ending is a homecoming. If it does not — if what is left of her is a reflex of survival rather than a wish for it — then the player spends seven days being recruited by grief, and the Stay ending becomes the honest one.
