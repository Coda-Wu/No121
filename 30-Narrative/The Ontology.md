# The Ontology

> [!warning] Top-secret layer. Never stated plainly in-game; assembled by the player from evidence, and confirmed only at the endings.

## What the world is

An artificial **vessel** — analog, administered, artisanal. **Not** a computer simulation; the banned-technique list in [[The Two-Grammar Rule]] exists to protect this. Microverse flavour (the car-trunk universe): the exit opens onto a single **scale-shock vista** screen, seen in [[Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach]].

## Anna is the Maker

The world is [[Anna]]'s, or entangled with her. Evidence accumulates — handwriting in blueprint margins, ledger gaps, the deed in [[DEED — The Past]] — but full confirmation is withheld until the endings, and each ending shades it differently.

## The two accounts of leaving

- **Anchor physics (the truth):** the world runs on [[Otto]]'s habitation and participation. As he binds to it, leaving = the vessel dying.
- **Decommission (the claim):** the Administration threatens "consolidation" in perfect legalese.

The player can **never cleanly verify** which story is true — the danger of leaving is documented only by the party with an interest in his staying. Silo machinery; evidence planted both ways.

## The Administration is triage, not tyranny

Sealed floors ([[The Sealed Fifth Floor]]) and midnight hammering are desperate patching, misdirected as menace — siege logistics against [[Mr. Vacancy]]. Work crews are only ever heard through walls. Management is never seen. Revealed in [[REPAPER — The Twins]].
