# Key Items

Eight items, one per chapter card after the cold open. Each is awarded for completing that chapter's main story. Together with [[Reverse Film]] they are what restores [[Anna]] — see [[The Body]].

**Reverse Film restores the body. The key items restore the person.** Reverse Film without the full set produces [[Do I Know You — The Hollow Return]].

## Sources

The eight are **mixed in kind — every item is a different sort of object**. Sources are: a developed print, pocket-puzzle rooms, and key NPCs.

**Exactly one** of the eight is a photograph. 🔧 Which one, and the source of each of the other seven, is Round 6.

## Her breath

The eighth item is different in kind from the other seven: it is what drowning removed. It is obtained by **burning the usher** — [[Usher Burned]] — in the final purge, and it is the only one that hard-gates [[True Ending — Informed Breach]].

## The rose

Separate from the eight. [[Otto]] cast one rose into the ocean each year for five years. The fifth reached her, and waking her is what strengthened her broadcast to him. She preserved it fresh with her own blood, sealed in a glass jar.

She gives it to him in the heart, in [[09 REDIVIDER — The Door]], as the revealing cutscene of that chapter. It is the only object in the game she deliberately hands over.
