# Key Items

Eight items across the seven cards after the cold open. Each is awarded for completing that chapter's main story. Together with [[Reverse Film]] they are what restores [[Anna]] — see [[The Body]].

**Reverse Film restores the body. The key items restore the person.** Reverse Film without the full set produces [[Do I Know You — The Hollow Return]].

## Sources

The eight are **mixed in kind — every item is a different sort of object**. Sources are: a developed print, pocket-puzzle rooms, and key NPCs.

**Exactly one** of the eight is a photograph: **her love**, developed on D5 from her own undeveloped roll on her father's rig.

**Nothing in this table is locked.** Each item carries a story, and that story is the main act of its chapter — the items and the plot are written together, not separately. The faculty names are placeholders until the real-world objects they attach to are chosen.

**Her childhood** sits with [[Pip]] on D3, and **her fear** with [[Nan]] on D6 — [[Room 434]] marks the moment Nan was physically dead, and what she is afraid of is being spiritually dead with nobody left to remember her.

Eight items across seven cards. The merged [[07 DEED — The Family]] yields **two**, one from each half of the chapter — [[Nan]]'s section and [[Renner]]'s. The count was not reduced by the merge, because neither NPC's act was cut.

## Her breath

The eighth item is different in kind from the other seven: it is what drowning removed. It is obtained by **burning the usher** — [[Usher Burned]] — in the final purge, and it is the only one that hard-gates [[I See You — The True Ending]].

## The rose

Separate from the eight. [[Otto]] cast one rose into the ocean each year for five years. The fifth reached her, and waking her is what strengthened her broadcast to him. She preserved it fresh with her own blood, sealed in a glass jar.

She gives it to him in the heart, in [[08 REDIVIDER — The Door]], as the revealing cutscene of that chapter. It is the only object in the game she deliberately hands over.
