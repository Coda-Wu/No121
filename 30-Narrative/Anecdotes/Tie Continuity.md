# Tie Continuity

A deadpan continuity gag across [[Leon]] and [[Noel]] — the twins alternate nights, and the tie does what ties do when nobody is tracking which brother wore it.

Doubles as a legible tell for the which-twin permutation axis in [[Permutation and Persistence]]. Formalised in [[REPAPER — The Twins]].
