# The Cigarette Gag

Escalates across all six days: the pack empties, and nothing is ever smoked.

**The last real cigarette is in the prologue**, at [[The Coast]]: he lights it getting off the tram and takes the last drag after the rose. It burns for the length of the beach traverse and is that scene's only timer. When he steps off the second tram it is still burning and it burns his fingers — a cigarette cannot survive that ride, and the pain is real, and neither fact tells him where he is.

Opens with [[Leon]] in the [[Lobby]] on [[01 №121 — Arrival]] — the cigarette vanish. Carried by the demo. **The vanish is the first treatment, not a trick:** the Building is curing his addiction. See [[Clause 01 — Smoking and Alcohol]].
