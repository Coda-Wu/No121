# The Opening Dream

Ratified in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. It replaces Dream 0 as [[Renner]]'s Calling.

The game and the demo both open here. **The dream starts with falling and ends with falling.** It is the tutorial, and it is the night in which [[Otto]] loses his memory and gains the face of the Agent.

Camera combat is not the core of the game. The dream teaches by a chase: movement, the camera, the flash, and the use of objects. It also shows that the Building is not regular.

## Beats

1. **The first fall.** He is falling. The player moves him in the air and learns to move. Shadow ravens charge him. Each one gathers itself before it charges, so there is time to dodge. They multiply.
2. **The flash from above.** A flash burns most of the ravens. He does not know what it is. It is [[The Camera]], glowing, falling above him. One raven draws back and waits for the last fall.
3. **The catch.** He takes the camera out of the air. This teaches picking up. He lands.
4. **The run.** More ravens come, and he runs. On the way the player learns:
   - **the flash**, which carves a shadow. See [[Shadows]];
   - **a light on a switch**, which holds a shadow in place. See [[Purge and Light]];
   - **surfaces that turn the floor.** He walks onto one and the wall or the ceiling becomes his ground. It needs no button. It works like Dreamwalk, with one difference: he cannot fall asleep inside his own dream, and there is no bench. See [[Abilities and Gates]];
   - **that the Building does not close.** A door leads back to itself.
5. **The exit.** The ravens flood in until the exit is the only bright thing. He takes it. It is a cliff, and [[Mr. Vacancy]] set it for him.
6. **The second fall.** The flock takes a part of his memory and tears it apart: his name, his trade, his life with [[Anna]]. His face begins to burn in black smoke. Then someone helps him. A strong burst of flash from the camera burns most of the ravens, which were copies. One survives. It is the true one. It sees what Otto could become, decides on a better plan for him, and flies away. See [[Mr. Vacancy's Plan]].
7. **The waking.** [[Room 202]]. See [[02 SPOOL — The Job]].

## Rules

- **Ravens only.** The raven is the one monster of the dream. Other shadows wait for the main game.
- **The player does not die before the lessons are given.** If he is overwhelmed early, or if the player stands still and does nothing, the dream slowly swirls and begins again from the last checkpoint.
- **After the lessons, ten hits wake him.** This counts as dying in the dream. A player who plays well meets real pressure and reaches the exit.
- **The game remembers which it was.** See [[First Dream Outcome]].
- **The dream lends the flash, and the waking takes it back.** On Day 1 the camera fires with the shutter alone. [[Eve]] returns the flash in Dream 1. The room behind the smoke is a stronger promise for a player who has already held the answer.

## What it changes in him

- **The memory.** He wakes without his name, his trade or his life with Anna. Finding it again is the work of Day 1. See [[The Memory Rooms]].
- **The face.** The Agent is Otto in his suit with a burning, smoking face. He keeps his reason. See [[Persona Law]] and [[Sanity]].
- **The sight.** The Agent can see shadows because the demon lets him.
