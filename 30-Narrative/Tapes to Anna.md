# Tapes to Anna

The nightly report. Third beat of [[Night Phase]]: develop → pin → **tape** → set the alarm → sleep.

[[Otto]] is near-silent in the world and voluble here — the tapes carry most of his characterisation, and most of his share of the 15,000-word budget in the [[Scope Ledger]]. Addressed to [[Anna]], who has been missing for fifteen days.

## The recorder is the investigator's tool

He reaches for it when he is working the case, and he does not reach for it when he is not. That is the whole rule, and the prologue states it twice.

- **Tape #0** — at the bedside at 01:10 on day fifteen, in `00a The Waking`. It teaches the record verb, and it is the **only recording in the prologue**.
- **The beach monologue is not a tape.** At [[The Coast]] he speaks aloud, to her, with no machine — because by then he has stopped being a detective, which is the whole reason she can reach him at all. See [[Timeline]]. It is the only time in the game he does that.
