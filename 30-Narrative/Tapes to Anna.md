# Tapes to Anna

The nightly report. Third beat of [[Night Phase]]: develop → pin → **tape** → set the alarm → sleep.

[[Otto]] is near-silent in the world and voluble here — the tapes carry most of his characterisation, and most of his share of the 15,000-word budget in the [[Scope Ledger]]. Addressed to [[Anna]], who has been missing for years.

## Authored tapes
- Tape #1 — [[01 №121 — Arrival]], during which [[Pip]]'s blueprint arrives under the door.
- Tape #2 — [[02 SPOOL — The Job]].
- The last tape — [[09 REDIVIDER — The Door]], one of the three disposition receipts. See [[Disposition Tracker]].

## Running gags carried on tape
- [[Noir Corrections]] — he corrects his own hardboiled phrasing.
- [[The Doorknob]] — dusting his own for prints, mid-tape.
