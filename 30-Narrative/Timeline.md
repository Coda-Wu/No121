# Timeline

> [!warning] Contains top-secret layer material. See [[The Ontology]].

## The four dates

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| **1991** | [[Mr. Vacancy]] takes [[Renner]] and [[Eve]], eleven years before the present, while [[Anna]] is a child. She is raised afterwards by [[The Aunt and Uncle]]. She never speaks of her parents to [[Otto]]; he never meets them and never sees a photograph of them. See [[The Curse]]. |
| **1998** | [[Otto]] and [[Anna]] meet. Four years together. This is the year the vessel is thickest — see below. |
| **8 August 2002** | Anna drowns at sea in a rip current while Otto is blinded by wind-blown sand. He clears his eyes to an empty beach. The wind is Mr. Vacancy's. Her body is never recovered. **The vessel forms in the same instant.** See [[The Death of Anna]] and [[The Body]]. |
| **22 August 2002** | The present. Day fifteen. The Coast Guard stood down on Tuesday the 20th. That night Otto casts a rose into the sea and sees the Building on the water — [[01 №121 — Arrival]]. |

Anna vanished on a Thursday and Otto walks out on a Thursday: exactly two weeks, the same weekday, and nobody says so.

## Two weeks, not five years

**The vessel does not accrete. It formed whole in the instant she drowned, and it has been failing ever since.** The rate of failure is the rate the body decays — see [[The Body]]. She is fifteen days dead, the adipocere has not closed over her yet, and the Building is young and already coming apart. The eight days of [[The Day Loop]] are not the tail of a long fade; they are the whole of what is left.

## Why it took Otto two weeks

Not because Anna grew stronger. **She has reached for him every night since the eighth**, and a lost soul is weak.

What changed is Otto. He has **very low spiritual sensing** — the exact inverse of Anna, whose strong sensing is what [[Mr. Vacancy]] wanted her for ([[The Curse]]). He is lead-lined. It is why the demon could blind him on the beach with a handful of sand, why it has never wanted him, and why she chose the worst possible receiver as her anchor: she chose him for love, not for suitability.

And the lead is **the investigator**. For fifteen days he has worked her disappearance as a case — the posters, the coastline, the hospitals, an address on a notice that nobody legitimate has answered. The professional armour is what blocks her.

**On the fifteenth night he takes her rose to the water.** Cutting a flower and putting it in the sea is not a search; it is a gesture, and it is the first thing he has done that admits she may not be coming back. For about ten seconds at the waterline he stops being a detective and is only a man who has lost someone.

**That crack is where she gets in.** The rose leaves his hand, the petals drift, and he sees the Building standing in mist on the water, lit from within, with the street number legible. See [[The Real World]] and [[Key Items]].

**Nothing official happens.** There is no letter and no court. Death in absentia is not issued automatically anywhere, and a partner has no standing to petition for it. The trigger is the rose and nothing else.

## What the dates buy

- [[Nan]] lives in **1998** because that is the year Anna met Otto — the year an old woman would choose to stay in. She is not the keeper of a founding; she is Anna at the age she never reached, still living in the best year she had. That is why the exit in [[07 DEED — The Family]] runs through her year rather than the present: **1998 is where the vessel is thickest, because it holds the most of him.** Her biscuits are best before 1999 because nothing in that flat was ever asked to survive past the year.
- **1991 is carried by paper**, never by a playable scene: the newspaper record of [[Renner]]'s fall, and the documentation of [[Eve]]'s death. See [[The Hidden Family]].
- A **2002** present keeps film cameras, cassette recorders, corridor radios and the tram honest without paying for a period piece — and it is the only palindrome year within reach. The analog register in the [[Art Spec]] and [[Audio Spec]] is contemporary, not nostalgic.
- Two invisible palindromes carry the prologue: the bedside clock reads **01:10**, and low water is at **20:02**.

## The real №121 (ratified as C1)

There is a real №121 in the real world. It is where [[Otto]] grew up, and where [[Anna]] was living when he knew her.

The vessel is **her memory of his stories about it**. This is why everything feels familiar to him and why nothing quite matches; it is also why the property deed and the records in [[07 DEED — The Family]] are literally meaningful documents rather than dressing.

**Otto's belief that he grew up in this building is false, and the player watches it curdle.** His childhood examine-lines — a reference channel in [[The Day Loop]] — are real memories in a wrong building.

## The dead (ratified as C2)

[[Eve]] and [[Renner]] died in **1991**, seven years before Otto met Anna. She built them out of her own memory rather than his, which is why they are the two residents who match nothing he knows, why they hold the deepest posts in the Building, and why their care for him is unexplainable to him. He has never seen their faces — there were no photographs of them in the house he shared with her, and she never talked about them.

The Building is populated by Anna's dead. **Otto is the only living thing in it.** See [[The Hidden Family]].
