# Timeline

> [!warning] Contains top-secret layer material. See [[The Ontology]].

## The two dates

| Year | What |
|---|---|
| **1998** | [[Anna]] goes missing and dies. The vessel is created in the same year — the death and the founding are one event. See [[The Death of Anna]]. |
| **2003** | The present. [[Otto]] arrives at dusk in [[01 №121 — Arrival]], five years after she vanished. |

Five years is the interval Coda ratified in Round 1: she vanished five years ago, and it took her consciousness that long to reach him. Everything in the vault dates from these two anchors.

## What the dates buy

- [[Nan]] lives in **1998** because she is the memory-keeper of the founding year — the last intact piece of the moment the vessel formed. Her biscuits are best before 1999 because nothing in her flat has been asked to survive past the founding.
- The exit in [[07 DEED — The Past]] runs **through 1998**, not through the present, because 1998 is where the vessel is thickest.
- A 2003 present keeps film cameras, tape recorders, corridor radios and the tram honest without paying for a period piece. The analog register in the [[Art Spec]] and [[Audio Spec]] is contemporary, not nostalgic.

## The real №121 (ratified as C1)

There is a real №121 in the real world. It is where [[Otto]] grew up, and where [[Anna]] was living when he knew her.

The vessel is **her memory of his stories about it**. This is why everything feels familiar to him and why nothing quite matches; it is also why the property deed and the 1998 records in [[07 DEED — The Past]] are literally meaningful documents rather than dressing.

**Otto's belief that he grew up in this building is false, and the player watches it curdle.** His childhood examine-lines — a reference channel in [[The Day Loop]] — are real memories in a wrong building.

## The dead (ratified as C2)

[[Eve]] and [[Renner]] were **already dead when Otto met Anna**. She built them out of her own memory rather than his, which is why they are the two residents who match nothing he knows, why they hold the deepest posts in the Building, and why their care for him is unexplainable to him.

The Building is populated by Anna's dead. **Otto is the only living thing in it.** See [[The Hidden Family]].
