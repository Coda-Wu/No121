## What happened before the dream

In story order. None of it is played in sequence. Each scene is a memory room, found later.

1. **The flat.** The PC that will not launch Queen, the radio, the telephone, the rose in its vase. He takes the rose, the tape recorder and a box of cigarettes.
2. **The bus stop.** He smokes, sees the MISSING notice he pasted there, and takes the bus to the cliff. See [[Street and Bus]].
3. **[[The Cliff]].** The snapped search tape and its light, and a raven on the post, watching.
4. **The cliff edge.** He walks to the end and jumps.
5. **[[The Rock Path]].** He wakes on rock in the sea and walks to the Building under the moon.
6. **The [[Lobby]].** [[Leon]], the cigarette that vanishes, the key to 202. The chain-locked well is walked past. See [[The Lightwell]].
7. **His first hour in 202.** The board unpacked: ritual pins and a sealed envelope marked "A." See [[The Corkboard Thread]]. [[Pip]]'s blueprint arrives under the door.
8. **The PC.** His first try at the Queen game, which he loses, and then sleep. This must come before any key item. See [[Her Side]].
