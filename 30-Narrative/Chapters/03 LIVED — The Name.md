## Structure

**① [[The Cafeteria]], floor 1 — the gut.** The chapter opens on a water reflection in a coffee mug, or a reflection in a glass. [[Noel]] is on the cafeteria shift today; [[Leon]] has the building entrance. This is the first time Otto meets Noel, and the chapter's first line is Otto's:

> "So... you're saying that you and Leon are twins."

A coffee-making minigame or cutscene, unhurried. Noel introduces [[Elle]], [[Nan]], and [[Eve]] in her ordinary [[Room 101]] form.

**② The corridor to [[Room 232]], floor 2 — the liver.** The room that was sealed behind dark smoke on D1 is here. Otto now has the flashgun ([[Purge and Light]]) and knows where to use it. This corridor teaches the two verbs awake: **the flash carves, the camera keeps.** The first lesser shadow is met here. See [[Shadows]].

**③ [[The Sleep Maze]].** A maze built around sleep and passing out — what last happened to [[Anna]] on 8 August 2002. It leaves Otto **Dreamwalk**. See [[Abilities and Gates]].

**④ Elle, out.** Otto drags Elle out of the maze and cures her insomnia. She has been sharing that nightmare so long that she knows what it is called: **[[Mr. Vacancy]] is named, from Elle's mouth.**

**⑤ The mug.** Elle remembers something, and quickly writes [[Anna]]'s full name on her mug — **Anna Salas**. The mug is the key item for **her name**. See [[Key Items]] and [[Elle's Mug]].
