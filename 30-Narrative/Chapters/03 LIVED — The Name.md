# LIVED — The Name

## Owns

The name. **This is [[Bob]]'s chapter.** It picks the plot up straight after the demo's 3 AM call.

[[Pip]] does not appear. His identity and appearance stay hidden until [[04 RADAR — Detection]], so the question of who is drawing the blueprints keeps accumulating.

## Structure

**① [[The Cafeteria]], floor 1 — the gut.** The chapter opens on a water reflection in a coffee mug, or a reflection in a glass. [[Noel]] is on the cafeteria shift today; [[Leon]] has the building entrance. This is the first time Otto meets Noel, and the chapter's first line is Otto's:

> "So... you're saying that you and Leon are twins."

A coffee-making minigame or cutscene, unhurried. Noel introduces [[Bob]], [[Nan]], and [[Eve]] in her ordinary [[Room 101]] form.

**② The corridor to [[Room 232]], floor 2 — the liver.** The room that was sealed behind dark smoke on D1 is here. Otto now has the flashgun ([[Purge and Light]]) and knows immediately where to use it. This corridor teaches the two verbs: **the flashgun disperses, the camera seals permanently.**

**③ The sleep maze.** A maze anomaly built around sleep and passing out — what last happened to [[Anna]] in 1998. The player must **fake being asleep while moving** from place to place, so that a nightmare does not find them awake and devour them. 🔧 Appearance and mechanism to be designed.

**④ Bob, out.** Otto drags Bob out of the maze and cures his insomnia. Bob has been sharing that nightmare for so long that he knows what it is called: **[[Mr. Vacancy]] is named, from Bob's mouth.**

**⑤ The mug.** Bob remembers something, and quickly writes [[Anna]]'s full name on his mug — **Anna Salas**. The mug is the key item for **her name**. See [[Key Items]] and [[Bob's Mug]].
