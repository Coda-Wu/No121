# DEED — The Family

## Owns

The family. **Merged from the former DEED and ROTOR cards** in Round 8: the whole family tragedy is condensed into the chapter immediately before the final night.

Playtime varies by player, by design — see the branch below.

## Structure

**① [[Room 313]], floor 3 — the eye.** Operation two on [[Pip]]; **NIR** unlocked. See [[The Cataract Operations]].

**② [[Room 434]], floor 4 — the ear.** [[Nan]], 1998, the property deed; the exit found through the past. The predecessor's ledger, last page. The newspaper record of [[Renner]]'s death in **1991** — a fall down a hill, recorded as an accident. See [[The Curse]].

**③ The bridge — the branch.** [[Mr. Vacancy]]'s delusion at the unfenced edge of [[The Lightwell]]. See [[The Delusions]].

- **Deceived:** Otto walks the bridge, steps off the edge, and the camera glass cracks in the fall. He arrives in the heart with a broken camera and the chapter ends here.
- **Undeceived:** he raises the camera in time; the white is wiped away and he sees where he actually is — the corrupted lightwell, with the phantom [[Anna]] floating in the middle of it. The chapter continues.

**④ [[Electrical Room]], basement — the brain and the nerves.** Only on the undeceived path. The descent; the junction reveal, wires and warmth, no gore, he still hums; the intercom retcon lands. [[Eve]] stands with Renner here. **The family truth.** The basement is clean of [[Mr. Vacancy]]'s influence — there is no delusion at the chapter's end. The basement darkroom station is found.

**⑤ The decided jump.** The tape is played at the chain — see [[The Three Hums]] — and it opens. Otto jumps carrying every key item, and the camera is intact.
