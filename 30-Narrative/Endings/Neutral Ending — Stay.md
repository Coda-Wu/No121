# Neutral Ending — Stay

**The socket.** The camera verb is to ***take*** the last photograph.

## What it means

**Accepting that [[Anna]] is dead — and never telling her.** She does not know, and in this ending she never learns. Otto keeps the secret, takes the post, and lives inside her belief. He **never returns to the real world**.

The socket is [[Renner]]'s. Staying is taking the father's post — joining her family. See [[The Hidden Family]].

Psychologically this is arguably the *good* ending: it is the only one where grief is accepted rather than negotiated with. It must be written warm and whole, or the triptych collapses. [[Nan]] is its best argument.

## Shape

The album mounted, the fuse seated, one final flash — his own last exposure. He never meets Anna.

Can trigger as early as the branch point in [[08 ROTOR — Renner]]; otherwise reached from [[09 REDIVIDER — The Door]]. Shaded, not selected, by the [[Disposition Tracker]].

## 🔧 Open

Settled in Round 3: [[Anna]] wants not to die alone, and does not distinguish between being returned to a body and Otto staying with her here. Both this ending and [[True Ending — Informed Breach]] give her what she asked for.
