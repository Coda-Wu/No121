# Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach

The camera verb is ***none***: the door is torn.

## Gates

**None.** Choosable in ignorance, always — this is the point.

**Second route:** if the player is taken in by the bridge in [[The Delusions]], Otto steps off the lightwell edge, the camera glass cracks in the fall, and he arrives at the heart without a working weapon. 🔧 Exact consequence pending the chapter-structure decision. ## What it means

The Building exists in a different space-time from the world outside. Breaching it **without knowledge breaks the integrity of [[Anna]]'s last fragment of consciousness.** The tear does not free him from a prison; it destroys her.

## Shape

The door is torn and **the water comes in**. The Building floods. **Both [[Otto]] and [[Anna]] die** — he drowns her a second time and drowns with her. In the last moment before he dies he can see her corpse, far off in the ocean.

The **scale-shock vista** and the crowd are **superseded**: they were written under the earlier ontology, before the drowning was settled, and no longer apply.
