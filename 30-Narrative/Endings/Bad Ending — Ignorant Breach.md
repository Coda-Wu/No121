# Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach

The camera verb is ***none***: the door is torn.

## Gates

**None.** Choosable in ignorance, always — this is the point. [[Mr. Vacancy]] as usher wants exactly this; an ignorant Breach does its work.

## What it means

The Building exists in a different space-time from the world outside. Breaching it **without knowledge breaks the integrity of [[Anna]]'s last fragment of consciousness.** The tear does not free him from a prison; it destroys her.

The **scale-shock vista** is the *consequence*, not the destination — one screen, no traversal, where the sense of scale fails and what registers as "a crowd" is the brain's first wrong attempt at something not crowd-shaped. The Building exhales out into nothing behind him.

Anna is conspicuously absent from that crowd, because she was never out there. She was the thing he tore.
