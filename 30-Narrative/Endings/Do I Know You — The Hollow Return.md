# Do I Know You? — The Hollow Return

The body is restored and the person is not.

## Gates

Camera **intact** — the undeceived path out of [[07 DEED — The Family]]. Then [[Reverse Film]] obtained and used **without the full set of [[Key Items]]**, in particular without **her breath**, which requires [[Usher Burned]].

## Shape

[[Otto]] restores [[Anna]]'s physical body. She has no memory of anything. The cutscene runs almost identically to [[I See You — The True Ending]] and diverges at the end: she looks at him and asks whether she knows him.

## Cost

Cheap to produce: it reuses the True ending's cutscene with a changed final beat. Added as a fourth ending by Coda's ratification in Round 4; the 3-ending lock in the [[Scope Ledger]] and [[Commercial Frame]] was amended rather than paid for. [[Penrose Stair]] is retained.
