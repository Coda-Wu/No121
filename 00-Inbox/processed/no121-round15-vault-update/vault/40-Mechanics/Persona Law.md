# Persona Law

> [!note] Revised in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. **The two selves are renamed, and the uniform is gone.**

The Shift. [[Otto]] is two selves and the game never remarks on it.

## The two selves

| | **The Suit** | **The Agent** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Normal Otto | Otto with a burning, smoking face |
| Old name | Agent Otto, the hub self | Handyman Otto, the floor self |
| Look | Black suit, white shirt, black tie | The same suit. His face burns in black smoke. |
| His verb | He talks and he reads | He sees and he acts |
| His places | [[Room 202]], the [[Lobby]], [[The Cafeteria]], [[Room 101]], the telephone, the PC | The floors, the mazes, the rooms behind smoke |
| What he has | The board. Residents who answer only to him. A Building that performs normalcy. | The camera's truth. The flash. The truth dressing. |
| What he lacks | He cannot see a shadow, flash or file. He can still be hit. | Entry to 202. Residents who speak only to a suit. |

**There is no uniform.** Playtest found it less appealing. The Agent wears the suit.

**The face was gained in [[The Opening Dream]].** It began to burn in the last fall, when the ravens tore his memory. He keeps the face as the Agent and keeps his reason. See [[Sanity]].

**The Agent sees shadows because [[Mr. Vacancy]] lets him.** See [[Mr. Vacancy's Plan]].

## Both selves must have enough to do

Round 15 removed the night routine, which was the Suit's whole job. The Suit is paid back in scenes.

- Some scenes are played as the Suit: the opening of a chapter in [[The Cafeteria]] with the twins, and [[Eve]]'s room.
- The mazes are played as the Agent.
- Each chapter note names which self plays which part.

🔧 **The Suit begins to see shadows from [[05 REPAPER — The Twins]].** Agreed in Round 15, to be tested before it is fixed. Until then the Suit is safe ignorance.

🔧 The ledger of selves across the eight chapters is a working draft. It needs more work.

## Trigger

The [[Elevator]], only ever the elevator. The service-lift ride is a threshold ritual and works floor 2 → floor 2. Never remarked on. Two gibberish-synth formants — the player *hears* the shift. What changes is his face.

## Memory model — THE HAZE

Knowledge persists; episodic detail blurs at the crossing. The artifacts are the reliable record: **photographs** are the Agent's memory, delivered to the Suit as prints on the board; **[[The Pocket Note]] and the board** are how the Suit speaks to the Agent. The two selves collaborate by leaving each other evidence.

🔧 Proposed in Round 15 and not yet ratified: the pocket note is written in the Suit's hand and read by the Agent.

Retro-charge: with hazy recall as canon, Otto genuinely cannot rule himself out as the taker of the un-taken frame — see [[Reverse Film]].

## Rules
- **Crossings:** satchel and camera cross; the change of self is automatic; no exchange room exists.
- **[[Stairwell]]:** never shifts. Blocked above floor 2 (WET PAINT) until ~D4, after which persona-routing is an earned advanced verb — choosing *who arrives*. See [[Connectors and Routes]].
- **Enforcement fiction:** no invisible walls. Doors and residents that only open for the Suit; wrongness that only manifests for the Agent. [[Room 202]]'s lock doesn't answer to the Agent.
- **Room 202 is the Suit's alone, and it is out of [[Mr. Vacancy]]'s reach.** It is the one safe room in the Building.
- **Doppelganger rule:** [[Mr. Vacancy]] always wears the self you are NOT. 🔧 With one costume, the two differ only by the face. Its two looks need to be drawn again.

## Terms

In this vault, from Round 15: **Suit** means the normal self. **Agent** means the self with the burning face. Older notes that say *Agent* for the hub self, or *Handyman* and *uniform* for the floor self, are out of date. The post that [[Leon]] gives, HANDYMAN (ACTING), is a job title and is not the name of a self. 🔧 Whether the post survives is Coda's.
