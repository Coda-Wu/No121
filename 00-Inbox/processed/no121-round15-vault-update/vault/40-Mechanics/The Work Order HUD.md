# The Work Order HUD

> [!warning] **Retired in Round 15.** See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. There is no work order in the game, in any form. [[The Pocket Note]] replaces it.

## Why it went

- A task now reaches [[Otto]] from a resident, from his own note, or from the place. See [[Requests]].
- The player is never sent to a desk to collect a sheet.
- A formatted order made the Building speak as an office. The redesign makes it speak through people.

## What the pocket note inherits

- **Docked and called.** It stays out of the view until the player calls it. Hold-to-peek on a controller. The design is cursorless, per [[Commercial Frame]].
- **Per-scene suppression.** It is hidden where immersion matters: cutscenes, dreams and [[The Ward]], the mind-spaces in [[The Delusions]], the bridge, [[Anna's Flat]], the underwater heart, and the climax of any pocket.
- **It writes itself.** Nothing on it is a player action.

## What does not carry over

- The list of 2–3 mundane jobs.
- The certification lines. See [[The Ruler]].
- The live count, IRREGULARITIES LOGGED: n OF m. Progress is read on [[The Corkboard Thread]], from anywhere.

## The rule it protected

**The blueprint is for planning and never for recording.** This stands. If a design ever requires the player to open the map to log a finding, that design is wrong. The board now absorbs all recording, automatically.
