# Connectors and Routes

From the redesign of [[Otto]]'s main world, 13 November 2026. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]], [[The Walk]] and [[Abilities and Gates]].

The Building plays as one map and not as six levels. Ways between floors are played spaces. They are never cuts.

## The ways

| Way | Its rule |
|---|---|
| **The lift** | The only way that performs the Shift. See [[Elevator]]. A new floor's stop is dead until he reaches the floor from inside. |
| **The main stair** | Never shifts. WET PAINT blocks it above floor 2 until Fold lifts the block. See [[Stairwell]]. |
| **The riser** | Behind service doors. [[Renner]]'s line opens one a day. The only fast route. See [[Doors]]. |
| **[[The Loft]]** | Reached by a hatch, with Dreamwalk. It drops into bolted rooms. The way into floor 3. |
| **[[The Back Stair]]** | Opened by Echo, and read with UV. It joins floors 1 to 4 and keeps his self. The way into floor 4. |
| **[[The Water Column]]** | Entered with the level. Water runs one way: down. |

Floor 2 is the main crossroads. The half stair that Fold completes is the way into floor 5.

🔧 The plan's section diagram counts six ways between seven levels. This table is read from the plan's text. It is to be checked against the diagram.

## Five rules for crossing

1. **Yesterday's ability is today's way in.** He enters floor 3 through the loft, floor 4 by the back stair, and floor 5 by the half stair. Catching Renner's line on the new floor wakes its lift stop. Each way is a space he climbs, rides or crawls on screen.
2. **The route chooses who arrives.** Only the lift changes his self. Every other way keeps the self he has, so a crossroads is a choice between the Suit and the Agent. See [[Persona Law]].
3. **Each way lands where the lift does not.** The back stair opens on the ring's far face. The loft drops into bolted rooms. The water column surfaces inside rooms.
4. **Water runs one way.** From the flood on floor 5 he can surface at any water below. From a lower pool he cannot rise.
5. **Echo finds a fork, and the filter settles it.** The Vacancy can forge an echo. It cannot forge what the optics show.

## Cost

Three new base scenes: the loft, the back stair and the water column. Each is reused on every floor it touches. The lodge, the post room, the pantry and the cold store are new small places. See [[Scope Ledger]].

The main risk is the level: every drowned room is a third dressing of an existing room. If the art cost is too high, keep the flood and the leak by 232 and cut the other two.
