## 🔧 Open — idle escalation

The dream and the hint system were **one system**: an idle night sharpened the dream, and a sharper dream was the hint. Each fruitless night added **exactly one** new detail. With no nightly loop there is no fruitless night. The rule needs a new carrier, or it retires. Candidates: the pocket note gains a line after a long stall, or Pip slides a new drawing under a door.
