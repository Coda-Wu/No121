# Abilities and Gates

From the redesign of [[Otto]]'s main world, 13 November 2026. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]], [[The Walk]] and [[Locks and Openers]].

Each maze leaves Otto one sense that is also a way across. None of them hurts a shadow. Each one weakens him while he uses it, which is what protects the horror. **Senses, never strength.**

## Rules

- **Two layers of lock.** Renner's line opens the way forward, one door a day. The four faculties, Dreamwalk, Echo, Fold and the level, open the way back.
- **Two tools from Pip.** Echo is sound: quick, short and loud, and the Vacancy can forge it. The filter is optics: slow, silent and true. Echo finds a way, and the filter settles whether the way is real.
- **Each ability is also a way of looking.** Dreamwalk shows what exists only in sleep. Echo shows what the walls hide. Fold shows what is not symmetric. The level shows what is not level. Each gives old floors a new class of anomaly without new rooms.
- **Every gate is seen at least a day before it opens.**
- **Day 6 adds no verb.** The end of the game is too late to teach one.

## The gate map

Sixteen gates. Each ability has one first use on its own day and two or three gates on floors Otto already knows. Floor 2 has a gate for every ability, so home is never finished.

- **[[Room 222]].** It opens to the afterimage of its own door frame. See [[Connectors and Routes]], chain A.
- **The wet-paint stair.** Fold is the reason the block lifts. See [[Stairwell]].
- **The water.** The level opens the leak by [[Room 232]], the cafeteria's dish pit and a puddle in the [[Lobby]]. From under the lobby he sees the chained well from below and cannot touch it.
- **New places behind gates:** the lodge and the post room in the lobby, and the pantry.

🔧 The full grid of sixteen gates exists only as a diagram in the redesign plan. It is to be atomized from there.

## In the opening dream

The floor turns on certain surfaces as soon as he walks onto them. It works like Dreamwalk, with no seat and no button, because he cannot fall asleep inside his own dream. See [[The Opening Dream]].

## In the demo

Echo, Fold and the level stay out of the demo. Their gates appear in it as closed markers, which is the hook.
