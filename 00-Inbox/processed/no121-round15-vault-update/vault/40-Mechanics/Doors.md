# Doors

From the redesign of [[Otto]]'s main world, 13 November 2026. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]] and [[The Walk]].

Most doors should not open, and only one kind should teleport. Seven kinds of door carry a floor. Each can be read from the corridor. A door refuses, shows, tests or teaches.

| Door | How it reads from the corridor | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Resident's door** | A palindrome number, a nameplate, light under it | A safe room: the scene, food, and a seat |
| **Vacant flat** | A palindrome number, dark | It never opens. The peephole shows one still room, and listening gives the static of [[Renner]]'s line. Side anomalies live here. |
| **Loop door** | Stands open; lawful | It leads into the corridor he just left, with one thing changed. He finds the change and photographs it. |
| **Wrong door** | A number that is not a palindrome, or one flaw such as a handle on the hinge side | The Vacancy's. It opens onto a shadow or drops him elsewhere on the floor. One frame closes it for good. |
| **Service door** | STAFF ONLY; it hums | [[Renner]]'s line opens one a day. It stays open and joins the riser, the only fast route between floors. |
| **Faculty gate** | Smoke, a hatch overhead, a papered seam, WET PAINT, standing water | It opens to one ability. See [[Abilities and Gates]]. |
| **Pocket door** | Unmarked, behind a room | The day's maze. The limit of six pockets stands. See [[Scope Ledger]]. |

The vacant flat is the cheap one. A peephole costs one image, hosts an anomaly and can look back, so most doors on a face can be of this kind.

## What a door hides

There are no upgrades to give. A room that opens holds one of six things. The rule against filler rooms stands. See [[Corridor Ring]].

- A box of film, two frames.
- A resident's food, which heals one hit. See [[Sanity]].
- A clause of the House Rules. A clause is knowledge that works as a key.
- A MISSING notice or a document for [[The Corkboard Thread]].
- One of [[Anna]]'s things. It adds a detail to the Queen game's idle screen and to the epilogue.
- A shortcut.

In Round 15 a seventh was added for Day 1: a memory. See [[The Memory Rooms]].
