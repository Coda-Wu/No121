# Tapes to Anna

> [!note] Revised in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. The tape is no longer a nightly step.

[[Otto]]'s report. He is near-silent in the world and voluble here — the tapes carry most of his characterisation, and most of his share of the 15,000-word budget in the [[Scope Ledger]]. Addressed to [[Anna]].

## Where a tape plays

[[Night Phase]] is dissolved. A tape is now a **tag** at the end of an episode, or a **voice over a cold open**. It plays only in the episodes where the grief thread has a beat. See [[Episode Structure]] and [[The Threads]].

## The recorder is the investigator's tool

He reaches for it when he is working the case, and he does not reach for it when he is not. That is the whole rule.

- **Tape #0** — at the bedside at 01:10 on day fifteen, in `00a The Waking`. It teaches the record verb. It is now inside the memory of the flat. See [[The Memory Rooms]].
- **At the edge he speaks aloud, to her, with no machine** — because by then he has stopped being a detective, which is the whole reason she can reach him at all. See [[Timeline]]. It is the only time in the game he does that. 🔧 The monologue was written for the beach. Round 15 moves the fifteenth night to [[The Cliff]].
