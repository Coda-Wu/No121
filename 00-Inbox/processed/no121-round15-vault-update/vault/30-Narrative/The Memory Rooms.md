# The Memory Rooms

Ratified in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. They replace the continuous playable prologue described in [[The Real World]].

[[Otto]] wakes on Day 1 with part of his memory torn away. See [[The Opening Dream]]. The arrival is never played in one piece. It comes back as **six to eight rooms of memory**, found inside the Building during [[02 SPOOL — The Job]].

## Why he has forgotten

The fragments are not only a way of telling. In the world, a flock of shadow ravens took his name, his trade and his life with [[Anna]] during the last fall of the dream. That is why the pieces must be found, and why [[Leon]] can point him toward a way of getting them back.

## In the flat

- **The radio** teaches the tuning of a frequency. It is the mechanic set for [[Renner]], met first here. It reports that the beach is still closed, and that the weather is strange: constant heavy rain and storm, and people are advised to stay at home.
- **The telephone** plays a voice message. The search has stood down.
- **The vase** holds the last rose. It is clay, from the real world. The jar in which [[Anna]] returns the rose is glass, from sand. The difference is meant. See [[Key Items]].

## Rules

- **Each room is found at an object** that belongs to its memory. Named so far: a telephone cable, an empty rose vase, and a place hung with tape and a light. 🔧 The others.
- **No other person appears, except Leon.** The isolation is the payload.
- **Little is asked of the player:** walking, handling objects, watching. All of the memories together take about ten minutes, without the Queen game.
- **Each one lands on the board.** [[The Corkboard Thread]] shows an empty pin for every memory not yet found. See [[Memory Rooms Found]].
- **The order is free**, except that the PC comes before the first key item.

## The tape at the cliff

[[The Coast]] forbids a police line, because a cordon tells the player that a crime took place, and [[The Death of Anna]] is locked as neither crime nor negligence. The rule holds at the cliff. What hangs there is the Coast Guard's search tape and its light.
