# The Threads

Ratified in Round 15 as the planning grid. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]].

The story is five threads. Each has a question, a carrier that the player handles, and an end.

| Thread | Its question | What carries it | Where it ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The search** | Where is [[Anna]], and what is this place? | The eight [[Key Items]] | The door, with the objects placed |
| **The residents** | What does each of them need from him? | [[Requests]] made in person | The three last conversations |
| **The account** | Whose version of 8 August is true? | [[The Delusions]] and the usher | The usher burned |
| **Her side** | Does she know, and can he reach her? | The calls and the Queen game. See [[Her Side]]. | The last run, and the parting |
| **The grief** | Can he leave with care? | [[Tapes to Anna]], the sea, the rose | The last tape, and the rose returned |

## Three rules

1. **One leads, one answers.** In each episode one thread takes most of the play and a second delivers the ending. The others receive one beat or stay silent.
2. **No thread leads twice in a row.**
3. **Threads cross at the object.** [[Elle's Mug]] is her request, Anna's name and the naming of [[Mr. Vacancy]] in one prop. Every key object should be a crossing of this kind.

## Use

This grid is for planning a chapter before its scenes are written. It is not shown to the player and it locks no scene. See [[Episode Structure]].
