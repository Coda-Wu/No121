## Beats
- Disposition receipts in three conversations: [[Eve]], the twins ([[Leon]] / [[Noel]]), and the last tape — see [[Disposition Tracker]], [[Tapes to Anna]].
- The usher: [[Mr. Vacancy]] *encourages* opening the door. Burning it yields the eighth key item, **her breath** — see [[Key Items]], [[Usher Burned]].
- **The last run.** The burning of the usher is played as the Queen's last run. [[Otto]] enters her game as a companion. His camera strips the Queen's shadow armour, and Alice strikes what is exposed. A cracked camera cannot strip it. Residents whose side requests were completed stand in the run. At 3:00 the run ends, whatever its state, and the two are parted. See [[Her Side]] and [[Side Requests Completed]].
- **The chapter runs through [[Room 696]] and then into the underwater heart.** The descent itself happens at the end of [[07 DEED — The Family]], not here.
- **The door ritual.** The key items are placed around the door. Pushing it open **without** placing them is offered as a explicit choice — a second safety — so [[Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach]] is always reachable and always an informed decision.
- [[Anna]]'s corpse is found there — see [[The Body]].
- **The rose.** She gives him the rose back, preserved fresh with her own blood and sealed in a glass jar. This is the chapter's revealing cutscene. The vase in the flat is clay, from the real world. Her jar is glass, from sand. The difference is meant. See [[Key Items]].
- **The endings.** Which are available depends on whether the camera survived the bridge in [[07 DEED — The Family]]:
  - [[I See You — The True Ending]] — camera intact only
  - [[Neutral Ending — Stay]] — always
  - [[Do I Know You — The Hollow Return]] — camera intact only
  - [[Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach]] — 🔧 always, or camera-cracked only; see that note
- [[Anna]] appears only inside the endings. In the last run she is Alice, so the rule holds to the letter.
