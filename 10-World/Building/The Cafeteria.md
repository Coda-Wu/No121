# The Cafeteria

## Function

The apartment building's cafeteria and restaurant, on floor 1. **The gut** in [[The Organ Map]].

[[Leon]] and [[Noel]] work it on a split shift: one twin serves [[Otto]] meals and coffee here while the other guards the ground floor.

This is the **social hub** — the room where most of the cast crosses paths, and where a large share of the anecdote-bank microbeats land. See [[Anecdote Bank]].

**No alcohol is served.** Coffee, soft drinks, mocktails, and whatever food [[Leon]] and [[Noel]] know how to make. It opens at mealtimes and it is the day loop's one pressure-free room: the chill moment each night.

🔧 The menu is designed night by night — what is served on which day is part of the calendar.

## The delivery port

**Otto can never enter the kitchen.** It is not his post: under [[Clause 04 — Reception]] a person may only be within a part of the premises they were received for, and he was received as HANDYMAN (ACTING). The kitchen door does not answer to him, and the game never explains it beyond the clause. It is deadpan and it is airtight.

So the **hatch is the only interface with the kitchen — and there is someone on the other side of it.** Whichever twin is on the cafeteria shift is in there; the other has the entrance. Otto talks to a voice through a slot and never sees the room. The tie-continuity gag runs through the hatch, and which twin is behind it is a permutation axis.

**The boundary rule.** Anything carried across the cafeteria threshold is taken out of Otto's hands and returned to the port. The port is the only channel, and it is also **traversal** — it reaches a place he cannot walk to.

## The escalation

1. **[[03 LIVED — The Name]], D2.** Entirely ordinary. He orders coffee; it comes back through the hatch. The coffee-making beat sits here, warm and unthreatening, and it teaches the interface without a puzzle attached.
2. **Later.** He sends something through and something else comes back — the tray returns with an extra item, or with his own item changed. No jump, no music.
3. **Later still.** Something rides in on the incoming belt. He flashes it ([[Purge and Light]]) and what is left on the tray is a key item.

## The payoff

🔧 Proposed, and the reason the lock is worth having: **the kitchen is empty, and always has been.** The food is real and nobody makes it. [[Leon]] and [[Noel]] are the Administration wearing two bodies — see [[Naming Canon]] — and the Administration is paper. [[Clause 04 — Reception]] is why Otto was never allowed to check.
