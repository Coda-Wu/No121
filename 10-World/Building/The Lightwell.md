# The Lightwell

## Function

The hollow centre of the ring. Visible across from every corridor face, on every floor, from the first lap onward. It is not enterable during the seven days.

At its base, on the ground floor, is a **chain-locked well** that [[Otto]] walks past repeatedly. It stays locked all game.

The lightwell is the **heart** in [[The Organ Map]].

## Entering it

Only in [[09 REDIVIDER — The Door]]. Otto climbs to the top floor and **jumps into the well**, carrying every key item, using the momentum of the fall to break through into the underwater heart.

[[Anna]]'s corpse is there — see [[The Body]]. The rose is given here.

## Dressing A (performance) / Dressing B (truth)

Seen from the corridor faces in both dressings, and treated as ordinary architecture in both. The underwater heart is a separate dressing entered once.

## Anomalies & cases hosted
- The chain-locked well as a standing, unexplained fixture from the demo onward.
- [[09 REDIVIDER — The Door]] — the descent, the corpse, the rose, the final purge.
