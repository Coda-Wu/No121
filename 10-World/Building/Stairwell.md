# Stairwell

## Function

The vertical route that **never shifts** — persona is unchanged by climbing. Blocked above floor 2 with a WET PAINT notice until roughly D4, after which persona-routing becomes an earned advanced verb: choosing *who arrives*.

Part of Agent Otto's night-roam territory with the [[Lobby]] and floor 2.

## Dressing A (performance) / Dressing B (truth)
