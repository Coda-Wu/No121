# The Water Column

## Function

The water that stands through the Building, from the flood on [[The Sealed Fifth Floor]] down. One of the ways between floors. Approved on 13 November 2026. See [[Connectors and Routes]].

[[Otto]] enters it with the level, on floor 5, and rides it down. He chooses where to surface: the leak by [[Room 232]], the dish pit in [[The Cafeteria]], or a puddle in the [[Lobby]]. **Water runs one way.** From a lower pool he cannot rise. See [[Abilities and Gates]] and [[Clause 10 — Standing Water]].

One base scene, reused on every floor it touches. See [[Scope Ledger]].

## Dressing A (performance) / Dressing B (truth)

One dressing. Each drowned room it opens is a third dressing of a room that already has two.
