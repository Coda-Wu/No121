# The Cliff

## Function

A coastal cliff meadow, in wind and rain, on the fifteenth night. New in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. It takes the fifteenth night from the beach of [[The Coast]].

Two drawn scenes belong to it. In the first [[Otto]] walks from the bus through the meadow. In the second he walks to the edge and **jumps**. Both are met in the game as memories. See [[The Memory Rooms]] and [[Timeline]].

## What is on it

- **The snapped search tape and its light.** He walks through it. **It is not a police line.** The lock stated in [[The Coast]] holds here: a cordon says that a crime happened, and [[The Death of Anna]] is neither crime nor negligence. 🔧 The scene is drawn with police stripes and a police light. It needs re-dressing, or the lock needs amending.
- **A raven on the tape post**, watching him. It is [[Mr. Vacancy]]. See [[The Delusions]].
- **The edge.**

## Dressing A (performance) / Dressing B (truth)

One grade: night, wind, rain.
