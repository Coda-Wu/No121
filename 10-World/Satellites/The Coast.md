# The Coast

## Function

The beach where [[Anna]] drowned on 8 August 2002, and where her body still lies — [[The Body]]. **One asset, re-rendered.** Every appearance of the sea in the game is this screen at a different grade, per the LUT rule in the [[Art Spec]], so it costs the scene audit nothing beyond the slot already reserved for the beach.

Long horizontal traverse, left to right: the tram stop at one end, the strandline through the middle, the spot and the waterline at the other.

## Dressings — three grades

| Grade | When | What it carries |
|---|---|---|
| **Night, low water, fog and easing rain** | the prologue, 22 August 2002, 20:02 | The rose. See `70-Script/00 Prologue — №121/00c The Beach.md`. |
| **Day, 8 August 2002** | interstitial after D2 — the search that first night | The game's only visit to the day she died, landing before the player knows what they are looking at. See [[The Real World]]. |
| **Mind-space** | the beach as [[Anna]] holds it | 🔧 Unplaced. Reserved in the Round 7 audit in `10-World/_Index.md`. |

## What is on it

- **Snapped search-sector tape** on a half-buried stake. Left because collecting it is nobody's job. **Not a police cordon** — a cordon says a crime happened, and [[The Death of Anna]] is locked as neither crime nor negligence. Otto steps over it rather than examining it.
- **The strandline.** He does not search it. He has searched it fourteen nights and tonight he came for something else; walking past it is the characterisation.
- **A bird awake at an hour birds are not.** Examine it and it flies off and [[Otto]] says nothing — the only examine in the game that returns no text. It is [[Mr. Vacancy]], as weather rather than as a body. See [[The Curse]].
- **The waterline.** Otto cannot swim. **The game never lets him cross it**, and he stops at the line every time the player pushes. It is the same wound as [[The Sealed Fifth Floor]] — the lung, flooded. See [[Clause 10 — Standing Water]].
- **The spot**, at the far end: the patch of sand where their things were on the eighth. The rose goes in from dry sand.

## The cigarette

He lights one getting off the tram — **the last cigarette he ever smokes** — and takes the last drag after the rose. It burns for the length of the traverse and is the scene's only timer, with no UI. See [[The Cigarette Gag]] and [[Clause 01 — Smoking and Alcohol]].

## Anomalies & cases hosted
- The mirage: the Building standing in mist on the water, lit from within, the street number legible. It fires when the rose leaves his hand — see [[Timeline]] and [[01 №121 — Arrival]].
- The hinge into the mind-world: close on Otto's face, the light changes, the tram. See `01 The Transition`.
