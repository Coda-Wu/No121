# Street and Bus

## Function

The arrival screen, and the only exterior. **One screen, two dressings, joined by the bell** — the bell is the transition from the real world into the mind-world.

**The transition:** night at the beach, fog. Close shot on [[Otto]]'s face. Camera zooms in on the face; background and lighting change behind it; camera zooms out, and he is inside a moist bus. See [[01 №121 — Arrival]] and [[The Real World]]. The building shows **five floors** from here, which is the standing lie [[Room 696]] contradicts.

The bus bell is one of the diegetic crossfade sources in the [[Audio Spec]], and it is the hinge of the transition above.
