# The Rock Path

## Function

A path of rock in the sea, barely above the water. New in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]].

[[Otto]] wakes on it after the jump from [[The Cliff]]. The rain has stopped. He walks along it to the Building, under the moon. It is met in the game as a memory. See [[The Memory Rooms]].

It is the first sight of the Building from outside. Its exterior is drawn as a European hotel. The Building blocks the waves that come from behind it.

## Dressing A (performance) / Dressing B (truth)

One grade: night, moonlight, the sea calm.

## Anomalies & cases hosted
- The arrival on foot. It takes the place of the arrival by bus in [[Street and Bus]].

## 🔧 Open
- How he came to be on the path. The game does not say.
- Whether he survived the jump. [[Timeline]] says that he is the only living thing in the Building. Round 15 neither confirms nor withdraws that line.
