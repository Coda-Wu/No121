# The Sleep Maze

## Function

[[03 LIVED — The Name]]'s centrepiece, and [[Elle]]'s. A maze built around sleep and passing out — what last happened to [[Anna]] on 8 August 2002.

**The player must fake being asleep while moving**, so that the nightmare does not find them awake and devour them.

## Entrances and exits

- **In:** through [[Room 232]], Elle's flat.
- **Out:** through the **smoke-locked door** on the corridor — opened from the *inside*.

The smoke door is seen in the demo and cannot be entered from the corridor, because there is nothing behind it reachable from that side. The demo's unpaid promise pays off as recognition rather than as a lock finally picked, and [[Otto]] drags Elle out through the door he first walked past on D1. See [[Purge and Light]].

## Payoff

Elle's insomnia is cured; [[Mr. Vacancy]] is named from her mouth; she writes **Anna Salas** on the mug — the key item for **her name**. See [[Key Items]], [[Elle's Mug]].

## Scope

Takes the pocket slot that was free. See [[Scope Ledger]].
