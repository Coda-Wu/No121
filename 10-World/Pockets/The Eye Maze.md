# The Eye Maze

> [!note] **Absorbs the [[Penrose Stair]] slot.** Round 12: rather than breach the ≤6 pocket cap, the Penrose pocket's slot is reassigned here. Penrose geometry is the canonical impossible-object projection, so the two are the same idea and only one needs a slot.

## Function

The floor-3 boss, and the second of two floor-3 puzzles — the corridors run the **afterimage**, the maze runs the **blind spot**. See [[Locks and Openers]].

**Isometric**, and a deliberate view break from the side-view corridors, the way [[Anna's Flat]] breaks to single-point perspective. The view **cannot be rotated**: one fixed point of view is the eye's condition.

## The core mechanic

**The blind spot moves with [[Otto]], not with the room.** Like a real optic disc it is fixed in the visual field, not in the world. Whatever falls inside it is invisible and cannot be interacted with. To see a cell, position yourself so the blind spot is somewhere else.

Isometric projection carries no depth cue, so **things that align on screen are connected** whether or not they are adjacent in space. That is the trick the whole maze runs on.

## Stages

Multi-stage, sequential; each stage exposes the blind spot once and then complicates it.

1. **Occlusion.** A tall block hides a cell. The blind spot travels with you, so the solution is a standing position, not a lever.
2. **The second spot.** A sick blind spot that does **not** move with Otto — fixed in the world, [[Mr. Vacancy]]'s. Two constraints to satisfy at once.
3. **The flash.** RMB floods the field and clears the blind spot for a moment — and burns an afterimage that becomes a new temporary blind spot. Timer-based decay, per [[The Day Loop]].
4. **The boss.** The Vacancy stands in a blind spot and can only be photographed from a position where neither spot covers it. **A positioning puzzle, not combat** — it never attacks, see [[The Delusions]].

## Reward

**Her childhood — a glass marble.** A key item; see [[Key Items]]. It sits in the maze's blind spot.

The corridor blind spot on the same floor hides the **UV filter glass** instead.

## Scope

Takes the sixth and last bespoke pocket slot, by absorbing Penrose's. See [[Scope Ledger]].
