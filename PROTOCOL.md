# The Corkboard Protocol — Lynchian sticky notes for №121

Lynch's card method generates ~70 disconnected scenes and lets structure *emerge*. №121's structure is already locked — 9 chapter cards, a thesis, three endings, a systems freeze. So the method is repurposed, not copied: **generation stays completely free; placement is disciplined.** The cards don't discover the skeleton. They put meat on it — and when one pushes back on canon, it goes through a gate, not around it.

The number isn't mystical here either. 9 chapters × (1 critical beat + 2–3 microbeats) + 9–11 clause discovery scenes + dream rungs + ≤6 pockets + tape beats ≈ **~70 authored moments**. Lynch's quota is literally this game's content budget.

## Statuses

`loose` → `pinned` → `placed`. Side exits: `cut` (kept forever — nothing is ever deleted), `canon-pressure` (contradicts a locked note → workshop decision, scope tax applies), `parked` (pitches a system, not a scene → systems freeze).

## Rules of the generative phase

1. One scene per note. Evocative title, not descriptive — the title is the first image.
2. **No placement thinking.** `when: unknown` and `where: unknown` are healthy. Guessing a day at capture is allowed, deciding one is not.
3. Fill only: title, What happens, Vibe, and whichever of when / where / cast / register you actually know.
4. Your prose is never rewritten by tooling. `/sticky` files it verbatim.
5. Stickies cost **zero** words against the 15,000 budget. The budget bites at the writing pass, not here.
6. Let fragments talk to each other: before a writing session, reread five random pinned notes.

## The loop

Capture (to ~40, later ~70) → `/weave census` → your cull (`loose → pinned`, `cut` the inert) → `/weave cluster` → you name the threads → `/weave place` → you approve placements → `/weave gaps` → aimed capture against named holes → repeat.

## Two filters worth memorizing

**The twist test.** A twist candidate earns a workshop slot only if it recontextualizes ≥3 already-pinned stickies without contradicting a locked note. Contradiction isn't death — it's `canon-pressure`, decided deliberately, deletion named first.

**Theme by census, not decree.** Don't declare themes; count images. Whatever concrete image keeps recurring across your stickies *is* a core theme — the motif table makes it visible. Check each dominant motif against the thesis (*leaving isn't wrong; leaving carelessly is*). If it serves the thesis, promote it into anecdotes and set dressing. If it doesn't, that's the most interesting object on the board — take it to a workshop before obeying it.

## Done when

Every chapter card has its critical beat and 2–3 microbeats sourced from placed stickies, every clause has a discovery scene, and the dream ladder is populated. Then the writing pass begins — outside this system, against the word budget.

## Quickstart

1. Unzip the addon into the vault root.
2. Claude Code: *"Merge CLAUDE-addendum.md into CLAUDE.md, delete the addendum, commit."*
3. Seed the board warm: *"Extract each chapter card's key beats and every locked anecdote-bank microbeat from the bible (atomized notes, or 00-Inbox/world_bible_v1.md) as stickies with `source: canon` — key beats `status: placed` with their day, anecdotes `status: pinned`. Then run /board register."* (~25–30 canon fragments, so new notes have something to react to.)
4. Write. Dump scenes anywhere — chat, `00-Inbox/stickies_raw.md`, or the template hotkey in Obsidian — then `/sticky`.
5. At ~40 stickies: `/weave census`, and follow the loop.
