## Store-page constraints

- Positioning order is **narrative adventure first, anomaly-hunt hook second** — key art that reads as a puzzle game contradicts the page copy ([[Positioning]]).
- First five tags: Psychological Horror · Story Rich · Pixel Graphics · Adventure · Mystery. Art should support all five and promise none it can't keep.
- Never name or imitate another game's capsule in a prompt.
- The final title is 🔧 open — №121 stands until Coda replaces it ([[Workshop Agendas]]). Compose so the plate can carry a different word without redrawing the building.

## Ending imagery — handle with care

[[Anna]] appears **only inside endings**, staged differently in each. Any ref of her, or of the scale-shock vista from [[Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach]], is spoiler material: file it here, never on a public board, and never in trailer or store-page candidates.
