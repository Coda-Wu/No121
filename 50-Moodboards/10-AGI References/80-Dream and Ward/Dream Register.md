# Dream Register — The Ward

Red-Room grammar. The dream register is the one place the game is allowed to be beautiful without apologising. Blocks in [[Blocks and Constants]]; [[The Ward]] and [[The Dream and Hint Ladder]] are the canon.

## W1 — the hub

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Wide shot of a single hospital bed in a room whose walls are heavy draped fabric, patterned floor running to the frame edges, no window, [Era prop], lit by [Primary light] from an unseen source, {PALETTE-BASE}, [Intruder], {MOOD}, flat frontal symmetry, generous negative space.

## W2 — the lightbox

The day's prints, seen as the dream sees them:

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Insert shot of a row of X-ray films clipped to an illuminated lightbox, the images inside them showing corridors and doorways rather than anatomy, lit from behind by cold even light, {PALETTE-NIGHT}, {MOOD}, straight-on framing.

## W3 — the leak (D4, [[05 REPAPER — The Twins]])

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Wide shot of a caretaker's flat whose walls have become draped fabric while the furniture stays exactly as it was, morning light through a window that should not be there, {PALETTE-BASE}, {MOOD}, flat side-on elevation.

## W4 — the calling (night 0, [[01 №121 — Arrival]])

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Interior of a basement electrical room seen as if remembered, junction box glowing faintly warm, cables running out of frame in every direction, no figure present, {PALETTE-NIGHT}, [Intruder], {MOOD}, one-point perspective.

## Escalation ladder

Each idle night sharpens the dream by **exactly one detail** ([[The Day Loop]]). Generate the ladder as a series with one variable moving:

> …the same room, [Escalation step], everything else identical…

**[Escalation step]:** one more film on the lightbox · the bed made where it was unmade · a second chair · the pattern on the floor resolving into a plan of the building · a door standing open that was closed

## Notes

- The Ward ships as an **LUT re-grade of existing screens** ([[Art Spec]]) — so dream refs should be recognisably the same architecture, differently graded. Do not design new rooms here.
- Waking [[Room 101]] stays mundane and belongs in `10-Environments Film`. The split is the joke: the infirmary is boring, the dream is the hospital.
- No glitch, datamosh, or scanline treatments — the dream is analog and theatrical, never digital ([[The Two-Grammar Rule]]).
