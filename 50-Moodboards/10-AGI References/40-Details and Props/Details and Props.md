## Notes

- Fidelity ladder is **eye < lens < print** ([[The Camera]]) — a prop ref is the *print* end. Expect more detail here than the sprite will ever show; that is the point.
- Text on props stays unreadable in environment shots but may be legible here. Anything with real words belongs in `50-Documents and Paper`.
- No intruder color on nostalgia props. Save it for the ones that are already wrong.
