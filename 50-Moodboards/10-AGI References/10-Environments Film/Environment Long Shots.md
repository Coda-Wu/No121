# Environment Long Shots — Film Track

Atmosphere, light, and dressing for the base screens. Blocks live in [[Blocks and Constants]]; `[Variables]` are yours.

## T1A — establishing

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Wide establishing shot of [Location] in an aging apartment building, [Architecture detail], [Era prop], lit by [Primary light] with [Secondary light], {PALETTE-BASE}, [Intruder], [Wrongness], empty of people, signage present but unreadable, {MOOD}, [Camera], generous negative space.

**[Camera]:** flat side-on elevation, like a dollhouse cross-section *(lean on this one — it produces side-view-native compositions that map straight onto screen layouts)* · one-point perspective straight down the corridor

## T1A-D — the dressing pair

Run the same prompt twice, changing only `[Dressing]`. The pair is the deliverable, not either half.

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Wide establishing shot of [Location] in an aging apartment building, [Dressing], [Era prop], lit by [Primary light], {PALETTE-BASE}, [Wrongness — omit in the performance pass], empty of people, {MOOD}, flat side-on elevation like a dollhouse cross-section.

- **Performance pass:** `composed and tidy, everything squared and recently cleaned`
- **Truth pass:** `the same room unperformed, dust in the corners, one thing subtly out of true`

## [Architecture detail]

> rectangular ring corridor seen from one face · perspective-rotating corner · doors set flush into the face · brass mailbox grid · numbered doorplates, floor-unit-floor · a stairwell that never turns

## Notes for this folder

- Room numbers and the №121 plate stay **Latin numerals** in every prompt and every ref — the palindrome canon is visual ([[Naming Canon]]).
- One ring floor is **one base scene** rendered as four faces ([[Art Spec]]) — generate faces, not new buildings.
- Era shifts are LUT re-grades of existing screens, so a 1998 pass on the same location is a *color* study, not a new set.
