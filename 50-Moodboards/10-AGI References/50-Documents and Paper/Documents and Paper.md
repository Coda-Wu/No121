## [Marking]

> a fluorescent smear visible only under ultraviolet · a marginal note in a different hand · a signature reading only an initial · a stamp landing off-register

## Register rules

- Legalese is **literature** here. Prompt for the *look* of a contract that someone enjoyed writing: heavy rules, generous leading, clause numbering.
- Keep text unreadable in environment and long shots; legibility is a deliberate close-shot choice.
- Chinese renderings of diegetic paper use 公文 / 合同 register — binding policy in [[Glossary]]. Refs generated here should survive that translation without redesign.
- Exactly **7** MISSING notices exist ([[Scope Ledger]]). Generate variants of the same notice, not eight different notices.
