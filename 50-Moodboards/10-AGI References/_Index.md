## Standing rules

1. Never name games, shows, films, actors, or characters in a prompt.
2. One `[Wrongness]` and one `[Intruder]` color per prompt, maximum.
3. Generate at 16:9, crop after. Study, never trace.
4. Squint-test character refs at ~90 px.
5. Log template + variables beside every ref you keep.
6. Binary images live in `50-Moodboards/_assets/` only.
