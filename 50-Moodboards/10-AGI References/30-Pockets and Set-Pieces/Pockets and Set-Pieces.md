# Pockets and Set-Pieces

The bespoke spaces. Each pocket owns exactly one gimmick, so each prompt owns exactly one idea. Blocks in [[Blocks and Constants]].

## P1 — the impossible corridor ([[Teaching Pocket]])

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Wide shot down a narrow service corridor that is longer than the building could hold, plain painted walls, [Era prop], lit by [Primary light] failing toward the far end, {PALETTE-BASE}, empty of people, {MOOD}, one-point perspective, the vanishing point too far away, generous negative space.

Companion — the nest at the end:

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Insert shot of a dark accumulation gathered in the corner where two corridor walls meet, [Material], lit only by a handheld torch beam from frame left, {PALETTE-BASE}, deep shadow, {MOOD}, low camera height.

**[Material]:** dust and hair · shed paper · something matted and dry · soot

## P2 — the mirror maze ([[Mirror Maze]])

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Wide shot inside a corridor of mirrored and glass partitions, a 4×4 grid of identical bays, reflections repeating out of alignment with the room, [Era prop], lit by [Primary light], {PALETTE-BASE}, [Intruder], {MOOD}, flat side-on elevation, one dead bulb hanging at the center.

The figure at C2 — always facing east, always wearing the persona [[Otto]] is not:

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Full-body strict side profile of a trim man in a plain black two-piece suit and narrow black tie standing motionless between mirrored partitions, face turned away from camera, seen through one layer of glass, lit by [Primary light], {PALETTE-BASE}, {MOOD}, entire figure in frame head to toe.

The contact-sheet escalation — three prints from one session:

> {FORMAT-FILM}. Contact sheet of three black-and-white frames of the same standing figure behind glass, the figure marginally closer and differently angled in each frame, sprocket holes and grease-pencil marks, lit flat on a light table, {MOOD}.

## Rules that bind this folder

- **≤6 bespoke pockets**, teaching pocket exempt; the maze family already claims three ([[Scope Ledger]]). Generating refs for a seventh pocket is a scope question, not an art question.
- Mirrored-tilemap construction is the default — prompt for **symmetry you could build from one tileset**.
- The Concession is invisible by design: never generate a ref that depicts a prompt, a hint, or an on-screen tell for it.
