# Floor Two To Floor Two

**What happens:**

The service lift is ridden from floor 2 to floor 2, and someone else steps out. The suit becomes the uniform. Nobody remarks on it; the formant under his voice changes.

**Vibe / dream logic:**

Lawful weird — clean, rule-bound, almost polite. A threshold ritual disguised as a lift ride.
