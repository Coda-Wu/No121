# He Wears The Suit You Left

**What happens:**

Three layers of mirrored corridor. He sits at C2, facing east, always, wearing the persona [[Otto]] isn't. Movement comes back mirrored; the axis remaps with every face you look from. Pin him against a wall to move him. A clear frontal photograph per layer is the way out.

Then the contact sheet, all three prints in one session: neutral — the eyes have moved since the shutter — closer than the glass allows.

**Vibe / dream logic:**

Sick weird. The parking brake is the puzzle. The west-face shot develops as the back of your own head.
