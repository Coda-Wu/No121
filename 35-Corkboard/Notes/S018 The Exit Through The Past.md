# The Exit Through The Past

**What happens:**

The exit is found — through 1998, not through the present. The predecessor's ledger gives up its last page, and with it the cost-evidence, readable both ways.

**Vibe / dream logic:**

Both readings are complete and neither can be checked. The party documenting the danger of leaving is the party that wants you to stay.
