# The Usher At The Door

**What happens:**

Final night. The doppelganger is *encouraging*. It wants the door opened, and it wants him ignorant when he does it. Burning it first is the difference between a homecoming and a tear.

**Vibe / dream logic:**

The antagonist's last move is politeness.
