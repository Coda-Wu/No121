# The Room That Went Out

**What happens:**

There will be a chapter in which one sick anomaly makes us use our flashlight to temporarily lighten up the room, as the room will be pitch black (caused by a power down). This sounds like the typical fuse problem in horror games, but let's make it different either in mechanism or visual appearance.
