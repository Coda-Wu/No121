# Develop-to-Heal

Photograph a **passive sick distortion** → develop it → the report is FILED → it is repaired overnight → muffled construction the next day.

**Undeveloped film reports nothing.**

## The mid-game realization

The hammering has been following your own reports all along. It reframes the Administration from menace to triage — see [[The Ontology]], [[05 REPAPER — The Twins]].

Work crews are only ever heard through walls; management is never seen.
