# Purge and Light

> [!note] Revised in Round 15 from the shadow feel test. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]] and [[Shadows]].

## Rules
- **The flash carves.** It is a cone of light. It removes the part of a shadow's body that lies inside it. It does not kill at once.
- **Half a body is the line.** Carved below half, a shadow lets him walk through. Above half, it only stands still while it mends.
- **Some shadows mend and some cannot.** A free lesser shadow grows back, like ink merging.
- **A holding light stops the mending.** It is a hard-sided column of cold light. A shadow inside it cannot move, and what is carved from it does not return. Carved far enough, it is gone.
- **Some holding lights are on a switch.** Thrown while a shadow stands under the tube, it catches it. Thrown at any other time, the lit tube is a wall that the shadow will not cross.
- **A photograph keeps** a lesser shadow permanently, and only from behind. See [[The Camera]].
- **[[Mr. Vacancy]] is immune.** It is only ever photographed because it permits it, and every print of it differs slightly from what the shutter saw.
- Each of the [[Filters]] purges a different shadow variant.

## Teaching order

**Revised in Round 15.** There is still no torch.

1. **[[The Opening Dream]] lends the flash.** The player carves ravens with it and holds one under a switched light.
2. **The waking takes it back.** On D1 the camera fires with the shutter alone. D1 ends on a **room sealed behind dark smoke** that Otto cannot enter: the demo's hook. The player knows what is missing.
3. **[[Eve]] hands him the flashgun** in that night's dream.
4. **The corridor to [[Room 232]]** in [[03 LIVED — The Name]] is where both verbs are taught awake: **the flash carves, the camera keeps.**

The system goes under pressure later in [[Flash-Survival Room]].

## Purge-pinning

A purged room's geometry stops permuting — a permanent record; see [[Purged Rooms]] and [[Permutation and Persistence]]. Home goes quiet, and the frontier does not.

## The final purge

Burning the usher on the last night is a gate for [[I See You — The True Ending]]. It is played as the Queen's last run. See [[Her Side]]. Unloseable: arriving unprepared throws you back, never kills you. See [[Usher Burned]].
