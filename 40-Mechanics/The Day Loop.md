# The Day Loop

The Hunt. Day = chapter = autosave at wake.

## Five beats
1. **Work order** — 2–3 mundane jobs; the route is where the Building misbehaves.
2. **The lap** — ring-corridor observation on the [[Corridor Ring]].
3. **Diagnosis** — lawful obeys a clause; sick violates one you know. See [[The Two-Grammar Rule]].
4. **Resolution verb** — **EXPLOIT** (use a clause to steer or traverse) / **PURGE** (light or photo, see [[Purge and Light]]) / **CAPTURE** (evidence, see [[The Camera]]) / **ESCAPE** (a pocket).
5. **Night** — see [[Night Phase]].

## Reference channels (anti-pixel-hunt)

Yesterday's prints (the album is haze-proof memory) · the House Rules · [[Otto]]'s childhood memory as terse examine lines · plain domestic sense.

## Day-advance rules
- **Only the day's critical beat advances the day.**
- Idle sleeps escalate: each fruitless night adds exactly one new detail — the diegetic hint system, unified with dream sharpening in [[The Dream and Hint Ladder]].
- Scripted blackouts only, 3–5 game-wide, film-cut style; [[Eve]] puts you to bed.
- **Timers are permitted where a mechanic needs one.** Revised in Round 12; the old rule was *zero timers anywhere*, and it is retired. There is still no timer on a day, a chapter, an exploration or a conversation — nothing that pressures the player to stop thinking. Timers exist only **inside** a mechanic that is about elapsed time.
  - Settled example: the **afterimage decay** on floor 3 is timer-based. See [[Locks and Openers]].
- **In-world clocks still lie.**

## Day math

**Revised in Round 8.** The target is **25–40 minutes per chapter**, across **8 cards** — the cold open, six days, and the final night. The cold open runs short and [[07 DEED — The Family]] runs long and varies by player, by design.

That lands the game at **4–5 hours**, raised from 3–4 h in [[Commercial Frame]]. The demo stays at **35–50 minutes** for the cold open plus D1 — deliberately shorter than the per-chapter target, because a demo that overstays is worse than one that leaves people wanting.

## Certification (ruler framing)

Some work orders demand certified measurements — MEASURED / SCHEDULED / DISCREPANCY / SIGNED, O. Most are boring on purpose. See [[The Ruler]].

## Meters

CUT as a mechanic. Survives only as deadpan flavour plus ≤3 authored visual beats: a wheel turning slowly backwards · dials at rest while the hum is loudest · warm glass.
