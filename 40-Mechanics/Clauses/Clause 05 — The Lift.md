# Clause 05 — The Lift

## Text (legalese as literature)

> The service lift shall carry one person only.

## Exploit (steering use)

The threshold ritual, stated as a rule. It is why the lift is the only trigger for the Shift and why it works floor 2 to floor 2.

## Sick-weird violations it diagnoses
