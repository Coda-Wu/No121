# Clause 01 — Smoking and Alcohol

## Text (legalese as literature)

> Smoking, and the consumption of alcoholic beverages, are strictly prohibited within all indoor areas of this facility.

## Exploit (steering use)

None. This is the one clause that hands the player nothing.

It reads as the most boring line in the document, which is its cover. **The twist: the Building has no outdoor area at all.** There is no exterior a tenant can reach — [[Street and Tram]] is the arrival screen and never a place to stand. So the rule is absolute, and [[Otto]] can never smoke and never drink, for the whole game.

This is the mechanical home of [[The Cigarette Gag]]: the pack empties across six days and nothing is ever smoked.

## The second twist — what the rule is for

The clause is not housekeeping. **The Building is curing him.**

[[Otto]] has been a heavy smoker for fifteen days — the ashtray beside the computer in the prologue is the evidence, and he is smoking indoors, which [[Anna]] never let him do. He lights his last cigarette getting off the tram at [[The Coast]] and takes the last drag after the rose goes into the sea.

Then [[Leon]] makes it disappear in the [[Lobby]]. That is not a magic trick; **it is the first treatment**, and the vanish that opens [[The Cigarette Gag]] is the clause being enforced on him before he has read it.

In the [[Night Phase]] seams of D0 and D1 the player gets it from the inside: stream-of-consciousness over tar, retching, and washing — the body being cleaned out. It is never explained and Otto never comments on it.

**She is trying to keep him alive.** It is the plainest care in the game and it arrives disguised as a prohibition, which is the only register she has left — see [[The Ontology]].
