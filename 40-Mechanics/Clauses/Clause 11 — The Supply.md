# Clause 11 — The Supply

## Text (legalese as literature)

> The supply shall not be interrupted.

## Exploit (steering use)

What [[Renner]] is doing in the junction, stated as a rule. He has kept this clause for years and it is the only thing he does.

## Sick-weird violations it diagnoses

- Any interruption. The pitch-black room in sticky S033 is in breach of this clause, which is what makes the darkness legible as an anomaly rather than as a set-dressing choice.
