# Clause 06 — Meals

## Text (legalese as literature)

> Meals shall be taken in the room provided, and nowhere else.

## Exploit (steering use)

Deadpan and almost entirely true. It is why [[The Cafeteria]] is the one pressure-free room and why the cast converges there.

## Sick-weird violations it diagnoses

- Food, or the remains of a meal, anywhere else in the building.
- Something eating outside the room provided.
