# Clause 02 — The Measure

## Text (legalese as literature)

> The demised premises extend to the measure last recorded upon inspection, and not beyond. Where no inspection subsists, the measure is that which the tenant paces.

## Exploit (steering use)

The load-bearing one, and the reason [[The Ruler]] is a system rather than a prop. A corridor is exactly as long as its last certified measure — and where no certification subsists, it is as long as you walk it. **This is why the tape keeps paying out in the Payout.**

Certify a corridor and you nail its length down. The demo teaches this fully, in the corridor behind [[Room 242]] that shouldn't fit.

## Sick-weird violations it diagnoses

- A space that changes measure **after** certification.
- A measure that disagrees with the record while an inspection subsists.
