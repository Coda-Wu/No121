# Clause 12 — The Well

## Text (legalese as literature)

> The well shall remain chained.

## Exploit (steering use)

Reads as mundane safety for six days. It is **the only clause the true ending requires the player to break** — and because the lock has no keyhole, [[The Three Hums]] is the lawful way to break it.

Found last, in [[07 DEED — The Family]]. Since ≥8 known clauses gates [[I See You — The True Ending]], a player can qualify without ever finding this one, and reach the well knowing the rules but not the rule that matters.

## Sick-weird violations it diagnoses

- The chain, when it is not on.
