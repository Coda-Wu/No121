# Clause 07 — Refuse

## Text (legalese as literature)

> Refuse shall be removed nightly, and shall not be suffered to accumulate.

## Exploit (steering use)

**[[Festering]] stated as a janitorial obligation.** What is not cleared nightly accumulates — and now the player can read the rule *before* they have suffered it. It also explains [[Develop-to-Heal]]: a filed report is a removal.

## Sick-weird violations it diagnoses

- Accumulation that has plainly gone unremoved for more than one night. Every ripened sick anomaly is in breach of this clause, which is what makes festering readable rather than merely punishing.
