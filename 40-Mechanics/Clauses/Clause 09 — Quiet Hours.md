# Clause 09 — Quiet Hours

## Text (legalese as literature)

> Between the hours of ten and six no tenant shall make or suffer noise. A tenant called upon shall answer.

## Exploit (steering use)

The source of [[05 REPAPER — The Twins]]' sound and mirror puzzles, and the rule [[The Couple]] are politely obeying every evening when they pause as [[Otto]] passes.

## Sick-weird violations it diagnoses

- Noise in the quiet hours.
- A door that does not answer when called upon.
- An answer with nobody behind it.
