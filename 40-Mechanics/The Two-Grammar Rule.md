# The Two-Grammar Rule

**Load-bearing.** Every anomaly in the game belongs to exactly one of two grammars.

> [!warning] Top-secret: the two grammars have two **sources**. Lawful weirdness comes from [[Anna]]'s decaying body — body horror. Sick weirdness comes from her death wish, bodied as [[Mr. Vacancy]] — illusion horror and unknown horror. **Both co-exist on every floor.** Each floor's anomalies are generated from that floor's organ; see [[The Organ Map]] and [[The Body]].

## LAWFUL WEIRD

The Building's own metabolism. **The organ working** — function performed correctly in a way no building should perform it. Clean, rule-bound, deterministic, almost polite. Governed by the House Rules in `40-Mechanics/Clauses/`. Never repaired, never festers. Photographing it counts as **documentation** — clause fragments in hidden print layers.

## SICK WEIRD

[[Mr. Vacancy]]'s intrusions. **The organ failing**, and beyond that the illusions the death wish produces. Rot-dark, asymmetric, rule-breaking. Its signature is **imperfect mimicry**: it imitates a lawful pattern with exactly one flaw ([[Room 696]] wearing a palindrome mask over 666).

Design note: blood and gore are not the horror here. The horror is the desperation of the death wish itself, and how the player is made to feel it. 🔧 Round 5.

**Diagnosis mechanic:** sick anomalies *violate a clause the player already knows*. This is why [[Clause 1]] is taught fully in the demo.

## The three dread registers, one owner each
- **Spatial / architectural wrongness (LEAD)** — owned by the Building.
- **Domestic uncanny (support)** — owned by the residents.
- **Clinical-institutional (support)** — carried by [[Eve]] and by paper. **The Administration itself is paper-only** (revised Round 7): it exists as notices and as the tenancy agreement behind the House Rules, and it does not appear, explain, or assert itself as a character. It surfaces in-game only where a beat explicitly calls for it.

`/weave cluster` reports register-ownership violations against this list.

## Tone dial

15–25% light, Lynch-style deadpan. Humour is situational and never winks. If deadpan fails in playtests, **cut toward 0%, never broaden**. See [[Anecdote Bank]].

## Banned

Glitch shaders · datamosh · scanlines · digital-simulation clichés. Wrongness is analog, architectural, bureaucratic — this protects [[The Ontology]]: the world is a vessel, not a computer.
