# Sanity

The player's condition. **Three hits.** Diegetic only — there is no health bar and no number anywhere.

> [!warning] 🔧 **New system under a standing freeze.** The [[Scope Ledger]]'s systems freeze routes new systems to the parking lot. This one was ratified in Round 13 by Coda's direct call and needs a cost naming. See the open items below.

## The indicator

After *Dead Space*'s RIG: the state is on the character, not on the screen. Three stages:

| State | Reading |
|---|---|
| Normal | clean |
| Hit once | **light smoke** |
| Hit twice | **burning black smoke** |
| Hit three times | fail |

🔧 **Legibility.** [[Art Spec]] puts sprites at 90–110 px, 6–7 heads, and the Eye Maze is isometric, so the sprite is smaller still. A shader confined to the *face* will not read at that size. Recommendation: play it at **silhouette** level — smoke rising off the head and shoulders, readable at a glance and from any distance — with facial detail as a bonus only where the sprite is large.

## Failure

In [[The Eye Maze]], three hits restarts at layer one. **This is not a death and not a blackout**: the scripted-blackout budget of 3–5 in [[The Day Loop]] is untouched, and [[Eve]] does not put him to bed for it. It is a soft restart, consistent with the vault's standing rule that the player is thrown back and never killed.

## It carries into the day

Sanity is not maze-local. It persists into daytime exploration and is restored the same way there.

## Healing

**Rare, story-significant consumables only.** Food, sweets and drinks tied to a character's past. **No generic drugs, no pills, no medkits, ever** — the clinical register belongs to paper and to [[Eve]], not to an inventory.

Everything needed for this already exists:

- [[Nan's Biscuits]] — mint condition, best before 1999.
- What [[Leon]] and [[Noel]] make in [[The Cafeteria]]: coffee, soft drinks, mocktails, food.

🔧 Open: how many exist across the game, whether they occupy satchel slots (3–5 per [[Permutation and Persistence]]), and whether using one has a cost.
