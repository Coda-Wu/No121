# The Walk

Ratified on 13 November 2026 in the redesign of [[Otto]]'s main world, and adjusted in Round 15. See [[Round 15 Decision Record]]. It replaces the five beats of [[The Day Loop]].

**Otto's day is a lock and a key.** He walks one floor to a gate he cannot pass, the resident's maze gives him the opener, and he keeps it. The main world plays as a side-scrolling Metroidvania with no upgrade tree.

## The beats

1. **The lead.** [[The Pocket Note]] names a person, a place or a thing. [[Pip]]'s blueprint shows the floor's shape today.
2. **The floor.** He walks the ring and the rooms off it. He reads doors, looks through peepholes, photographs the day's anomalies and meets at most three shadows. See [[Corridor Ring]], [[Doors]] and [[Shadows]].
3. **The gate he cannot pass.** One obstacle of a new kind stands on the route. He can see past it, and nothing he carries opens it.
4. **The resident and the maze.** The scene, then the day's maze. The maze teaches one verb in its pure form.
5. **The verb comes home.** He leaves the maze with that verb as a lasting ability and uses it at once on the gate from beat 3. A shortcut back to the lift lies behind it. See [[Abilities and Gates]].
6. **The cut.** The episode ends on its act-out. There is no played night. The day's key item goes on the desk beside the PC in [[Room 202]]. See [[Episode Structure]] and [[Her Side]].

From Day 3 the walk begins on an older floor, because yesterday's ability is the way into today's floor. It also passes one optional older gate, a detour of two to four minutes. See [[Connectors and Routes]].

## Looking replaces measuring

1. **Blueprint against corridor.** Pip's crayon plan is true and the corridor may differ from it. See [[Clause 03 — The Later Plan]].
2. **Door numbers.** A palindrome belongs to the Building. Any other number is the Vacancy's.
3. **The lens.** The viewfinder glows toward the day's main anomaly only.
4. **The new sense.** From Day 2, each ability re-reads screens that already exist.

## A floor

The ring stays. It always returns him to the lift and costs one base scene. A floor is four faces, three to five rooms that open, one pocket, one gate for today and one gate for a later day.

## Limits

- **Shadows:** three a walk at most, and no more than a fifth of the walk's minutes.
- **Senses, never strength.** Otto may gain ways of seeing and crossing. He never gains damage, health or film.
- **Length:** the walk fits the 25 to 40 minutes of a chapter.
- **No backtrack takes more than 90 seconds** from a lift stop or a service door.

## Round 15 adjustments

- Beat 1 was "the note names a room and a job". There are no jobs now. See [[Requests]].
- Beat 6 was the night. It is now a cut.
- Each chapter opens inside its plot, so a walk need not start at 202.
