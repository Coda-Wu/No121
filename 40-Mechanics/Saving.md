# Saving

Replaces the old one-line rule, *day = chapter = autosave at wake*, which was written when chapters ran 21–35 minutes and there were nine of them. At 25–40 minutes per chapter — and with [[07 DEED — The Family]] running longer still and varying by player — one save point per chapter loses too much.

## Three granularities

**① Beat autosave — invisible, and the one that matters.**
The game saves silently at every beat boundary: a puzzle solved, a scene entered or left, a pocket cleared, an item taken, a conversation finished. The player never thinks about it and never loses more than a few minutes.

**② Save and Quit.**
An explicit option that writes the current state and exits. It is how a player stops mid-chapter on purpose. It is not a slot; it resumes and is consumed.

**③ Chapter select.**
The wake points stay as they always were, and become the replay surface. With four endings and gated content this is what makes a second run cheap to reach.

## What is deliberately absent



The branch at the bridge in [[07 DEED — The Family]] is the most consequential action in the game: it decides whether the camera survives, and therefore whether [[I See You — The True Ending]] and [[Do I Know You — The Hollow Return]] remain reachable.

The game now has multiple slots plus an autosave.

**There are save shrine but very limited during daytime exploration, and autosave takes cares of most saving'**


## The diegetic touch

The flavour goes on the indicator instead of on a device. When the game saves, **the corner of the work order lifts and settles** at its docked edge — see [[The Work Order HUD]]. No icon, no word, no spinner. It reads as paper.

## What the save must contain

Beyond flags and inventory: the permutation state read at bedtime — satchel category carried, last door used, alarm-clock state, last conversation and which twin — per [[Permutation and Persistence]]. A mid-chapter save has to carry these or the night phase reads wrong on resume.

Also: [[Sanity]] state, the album, [[The Corkboard Thread]] and its drawn threads, and which of the four conclusions have been reached.
