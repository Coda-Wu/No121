# MISSING Notices Collected

## What the Building remembers

How many of the **7** MISSING notices [[Otto]] has pocketed. Seven exactly — a scope lock in the [[Scope Ledger]].

**Otto printed and posted these himself, in the real world**, in the two weeks since she vanished. All seven are for [[Anna]], each slightly different in wording, in the photograph used, and in the date. He is confused the first time he finds one inside the Building, because he does not remember posting it here.

**They are the wall between the two worlds.** His relentless searching is what keeps the connection possible at all; the notices are what that search left behind. Collecting them shows Anna's spirit that he did look. See [[The Real World]] and [[Timeline]].

**≥5 of 7** is half the [[Reverse Film]] grant.

**The lock is Building-only.** The poster Otto flattens at the tram stop in the prologue is a real-world poster on a real wall and does not count against the seven — it is where the player first reads her name and sees her face. See [[The Real World]].
