# Filters

Screw-on filters for [[The Camera]]. Cheap revisit content: each one re-reads screens that already exist.

## How they are obtained

**The camera does not ship with filters.** Each one is unlocked by an operation on [[Pip]] — a shard of that filter's glass is implanted in one of his eyes, and the camera gains the capability. See [[The Cataract Operations]].

| Filter | Unlocked | By |
|---|---|---|
| **UV** | mid D3 | operation one |
| **NIR** | mid D6 | operation two |

**The quest flow, ratified Round 13.** [[Eve]] asks [[Otto]] to **find the filter first** and does not say why. He retrieves the UV glass from the corridor blind spot on floor 3 — see [[The Eye Maze]]. **Only after it is in his hand** does she explain what it is for and what has to be done with it: for the truth to be visible, the camera and [[Pip]] must share the same view.

🔧 Where the NIR glass comes from is unassigned.

**UV and NIR mechanics are postponed** until the lawful anomalies are settled for every floor. Nothing below is designed.

## UV

The fluorescent residue of the Settling-pollution, on **the building and on objects**.

**UV does not scan files or documents**, except inside specific authored minigames. Revised in Round 7: the player has to be able to trust what the filter is for.

Its first use is turned against Otto — see [[The Delusions]], D3.

## NIR

The original architecture under the wallpaper — **the true floor plan**. A gate for [[I See You — The True Ending]]; tracked as [[NIR True Plan Seen]].

NIR is also how [[Otto]] can tell a delusion from the thing itself — see [[Mr. Vacancy]].

## Both

Each filter also purges a different shadow variant — see [[Purge and Light]].
