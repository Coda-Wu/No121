# Filters

Screw-on filters for [[The Camera]]. Cheap revisit content: each one re-reads screens that already exist.

## How they are obtained

**The camera does not ship with filters.** Each one is unlocked by an operation on [[Pip]] — a shard of that filter's glass is implanted in one of his eyes, and the camera gains the capability. See [[The Cataract Operations]].

| Filter | Unlocked | By |
|---|---|---|
| **UV** | mid D3 | operation one |
| **NIR** | mid D6 | operation two |

[[Eve]] supplies the filter and the reason: for the truth to be visible, the camera and Pip must share the same view.

## UV

The fluorescent residue of the Settling-pollution, and **hidden marginalia on documents already read**.

## NIR

The original architecture under the wallpaper — **the true floor plan**. A gate for [[True Ending — Informed Breach]]; tracked as [[NIR True Plan Seen]].

NIR is also how [[Otto]] can tell a delusion from the thing itself — see [[Mr. Vacancy]].

## Both

Each filter also purges a different shadow variant — see [[Purge and Light]].
