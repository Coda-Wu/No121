# Disposition Tracker

Reads satchel habits, door habits, and treatment of residents.

**It never selects an ending.** The gate is elsewhere: the bridge in [[07 DEED — The Family]] decides whether the camera survives, and that is what removes [[I See You — The True Ending]] and [[Do I Know You — The Hollow Return]] from the table. It is the single most consequential player action in the game.

The tracker shades:
- the final night's three conversations — [[Eve]], the twins ([[Leon]] / [[Noel]]), and the last tape in [[Tapes to Anna]];
- the epilogue.

Disposition never removes an ending. Which endings are on the table at [[08 REDIVIDER — The Door]] depends on the camera, not on attitude — see [[07 DEED — The Family]]. [[Neutral Ending — Stay]] and [[Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach]] are always available; the other two need an intact camera.
