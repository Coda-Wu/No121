# Persona Law

The Shift. [[Otto]] is two selves and the game never remarks on it.

## The two selves
- **Agent Otto** (suit) — hub self. Development, [[The Corkboard Thread]], [[Tapes to Anna]], night-roam (floor 2, [[Stairwell]], [[Lobby]]). The Building *performs normalcy* for the Agent: same tilemap, composed dressing.
- **Handyman Otto** (uniform) — floor self. Work orders, instruments, purging, pockets. Sees the truth dressing.

## Trigger

The [[Elevator]], only ever the elevator. The service-lift ride is a threshold ritual and works floor 2 → floor 2. Never shown as a costume change; never remarked on. Two gibberish-synth formants — the player *hears* the shift.

## Memory model — THE HAZE

Knowledge persists; episodic detail blurs at the crossing. The artifacts are the reliable record: **photographs** are the Handyman's memory delivered to the Agent in the developing tray; **work orders and the corkboard** are the Agent's instructions passed back down. The two selves collaborate by leaving each other evidence.

Retro-charge: with hazy recall as canon, Otto genuinely cannot rule himself out as the taker of the un-taken frame — see [[Reverse Film]].

## Rules
- **Crossings:** satchel and camera cross; suit ⇄ uniform is automatic; no exchange room exists.
- **[[Stairwell]]:** never shifts. Blocked above floor 2 (WET PAINT) until ~D4, after which persona-routing is an earned advanced verb — choosing *who arrives*.
- **Enforcement fiction:** no invisible walls. Doors and residents that only open for a suit; wrongness that only manifests for a uniform. [[Room 202]]'s lock doesn't answer to the uniform, which closes the skip-the-ritual hole.
- **Doppelganger rule:** [[Mr. Vacancy]] always wears the persona you are NOT.
