# The Three Hums

What releases the chain on the well at the base of [[The Lightwell]]. It replaces the Master Fuse, retired in Round 9 — see [[Master Fuse Seated]].

## The lock

**The lock on the chain has no keyhole.** The player is meant to see that and understand that no key exists for it, and that the mechanism is something else.

## The tape

Three hums — **[[Renner]]'s, [[Eve]]'s, and [[Otto]]'s** — recorded separately and **mixed down onto one tape**, then played near the chain.

Renner already hums in the junction, and has been every radio and every crackle under a door all game. The other two have to be got.

## Where it comes from

[[07 DEED — The Family]], out of the basement maze. The organ there is the **brain and the nervous system**, so the maze is built around signal rather than around locks.

🔧 Undesigned: how the player finds three recorders or three chances to record, how the mixdown is performed, and what the maze's verbs are.
