# Pip

## Role

The Child Cartographer of [[Room 313]]. An infant who crawls the corridors and drafts like a municipal architect.

**A gender-neutral baby.** Never assigned a sex, drawn without one, and voiced with male and female tones mixed together.

> [!warning] Top-secret: **Pip is the manifestation of [[Anna]]'s childhood.** Not a person she knew and not a relative — one of her own states. See [[The Hidden Family]]. Only the child's eye can see things clearly, which is why Pip draws the true map and the Administration's Schedule lies.

**Pip is blind** — cataracts, which are [[Anna]]'s — and **too young to speak**. Pip makes noise; Pip does not talk, comment, rate or judge. The only channel is the drawings, and even those carry very little text.

Sends the master blueprint under the door on night 0, mid-tape; refined copies every morning. The blueprint is the map surface — see [[Night Phase]].

Under [[The Organ Map]], floor 3 is the **eye**. [[Otto]] performs **two cataract operations**, one per eye, mid D3 and at the top of D6 — see [[The Cataract Operations]]. Each implants a shard of a camera filter and unlocks that filter's capability; see [[Filters]].

**No surname.** Pip is never given one and the question is never raised in game — see [[Naming Canon]].
