# Nan

## Role

The Grandmother of [[Room 434]], still living in 1998. The domestic register's warm heart, and the Stay ending's best argument.

**The memory-keeper.** 1998 is the founding year — the year [[Anna]] died and the vessel formed, in one event ([[Timeline]]). Nan is not stuck in 1998 out of whimsy: she is the last intact piece of the founding moment, which is why the exit in [[07 DEED — The Past]] runs through her year rather than through the present, and why nothing in her flat was ever asked to survive past it.
