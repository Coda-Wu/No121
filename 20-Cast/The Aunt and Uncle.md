# The Aunt and Uncle

## Role

Real-world only. They raised [[Anna]] after [[Renner]] and [[Eve]] were taken in **1991**, when she was a child — see [[The Curse]] and [[Timeline]].

**They are never named and never appear.** No portrait, no sprite, no voice. They exist so that one fact can exist: **Anna grew up without her parents in the house, and never spoke about them.** [[Otto]] has met the aunt and uncle. He has never met Renner or Eve, and never saw a photograph of them.

That is the whole load they carry, and it is a heavy one — it is why Otto cannot place her father in the [[Electrical Room]] or her mother in [[The Ward]], and why their care for him is unexplainable to him. Without it, his failure to recognise them is a plot convenience. With it, it is a fact about his life.

## Where the player meets the idea

**One framed photograph** in the flat, in the prologue: Anna as a child with two adults who are plainly not her parents. Optional, missable, and it carries no premise — only colour. See [[The Script Protocol]] on what may and may not be missable.

Otto's examine line does the rest: her parents died when she was small, an aunt and uncle raised her, she never talked about them, he never met them.

**No photograph of Renner or Eve exists anywhere in the flat.** That absence is the actual delivery; the framed photo just makes it legible.

## Relations
- [[Anna]] — they raised her.
- [[Renner]] and [[Eve]] — her parents, dead before the aunt and uncle took her in. See [[The Hidden Family]].
- [[Otto]] — has met them; they are the only family of hers he knows.
