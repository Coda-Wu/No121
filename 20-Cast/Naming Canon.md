# Naming Canon

Diegetic rule, **never stated aloud** — discoverable via the lobby mailbox grid and the handyman roster.

## The rule

Palindrome names mark those who are *of the Building*: [[Otto]], [[Anna]], [[Eve]], [[Elle]], [[Pip]], [[Nan]], [[Renner]] — with [[Leon]] & [[Noel]] as a two-body loop.

Room numbers are floor-unit-floor palindromes: [[Room 202]], [[Room 212]], [[Room 232]], [[Room 313]], [[Room 434]]; the infirmary is [[Room 101]]; the street is №121.

## Family names

**Only the Salas family carries one.** *Salas* — Spanish for rooms, and a palindrome — belongs to [[Anna]], [[Renner]] and [[Eve]].

**[[Pip]], [[Nan]] and [[Elle]] are never given surnames and the question is never raised in game.** No deed, contract or register names them; giving either a family name would close down the reading that they are states of Anna rather than relatives. [[Otto]] has no surname yet.

Anna's full name is first written on [[Elle's Mug]] in [[03 LIVED — The Name]].

## The violations that prove it

[[Mr. Vacancy]]'s non-palindrome, and [[Room 696]] reading 666 where the nail has fallen.

## The secret layer

> [!warning] Top-secret. A palindrome name marks something **of [[Anna]]** — either a person she knew in life, or one of her own states. See [[The Hidden Family]], [[The Ontology]].

Three categories:

- **People she knew:** [[Otto]] ✓ her partner · [[Eve]] ✓ her mother · [[Renner]] ✓ her father.
- **States of herself:** [[Pip]] ✓ her childhood · [[Anna]] ✓ the present · [[Nan]] ✓ the age she never reached.
- **Faculties of herself:** [[Elle]] ✓ her dream.

Every palindrome in the game is now accounted for.
- [[Leon]] and [[Noel]] carry no palindrome individually because they are not drawn from anyone: they are Anna's own institutional register, the Administration wearing two bodies. Their loop is a form, not a person.
- [[The Couple]] carry none: wall-voices, not originals.
- [[Mr. Vacancy]] carries none because it knew nobody. That is the real reason the inhuman thing doesn't get a loop-name, and why it keeps trying to fake one.

## Restraint amendment (R9)

The palindrome canon is load-bearing in character names, room numbers, and the settled chapter cards — and deliberately restrained everywhere else. New names (title, achievements, items, signage) do **not** pile on further palindromes. The pattern stays special because it stays scarce.

## Accounting note (R9)

The ≤8 cast lock was written when portraits existed. With bubble dialogue the binding constraint is the 15,000-word budget in the [[Scope Ledger]], which governs all speakers including [[Renner]] and [[The Couple]]. Voice-formant count (~10 gibberish-synth presets) is a trivial cost.
