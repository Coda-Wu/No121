# The Hidden Family

> [!warning] Top-secret layer. **Never disclosed plainly in-game**, at any point, in any ending. See [[The Ontology]] and [[Timeline]].

## The map

| In the Building | In life | Guards her |
|---|---|---|
| [[Eve]] — the Nurse, [[Room 101]] and [[The Ward]] | [[Anna]]'s **mother** | **Psychologically.** She tells the truth about the death and is disbelieved. |
| [[Renner]] — the predecessor handyman, merged into the [[Electrical Room]] junction | [[Anna]]'s **father** | **Physically.** He held the post until he could no longer, and spent himself doing it. Love carried beyond death. |
| [[Otto]] | Her partner — the only living thing in the Building | Both, badly, and by her invitation. |

Both parents died before Otto ever met Anna (ratified as C2). She built them from **her** memory rather than his, which is why they match nothing he knows, why they hold the deepest posts, and why their care for him has no explanation he can reach.

**They were all taken by [[Mr. Vacancy]].** The family was cursed and Anna was the last one it wanted — see [[The Curse]].

## Why this changes what is already written

- [[Eve]]'s "visiting hours end at three" is a mother running a hospice, telling him how long her daughter has.
- Every scripted blackout ending with Eve having put Otto to bed is a mother caring for her daughter's partner.
- [[Renner]] still hums in the junction because he is comfortable — a father keeping the lights on for her. The horror is that it isn't horrible.
- The socket in [[Neutral Ending — Stay]] is **Otto taking the father's post**. Staying is joining her family.
- The camera's requisition slip, RETURN UPON TERMINATION OF TENANCY, is her father's tool handed down.

## The states and the faculty

[[Pip]] and [[Nan]] are **not people Anna knew.** They are Anna herself at other ages, after the Buddhist figure of the past, the present and the future:

| | |
|---|---|
| [[Pip]] | her childhood — a gender-neutral infant, blind with her cataracts, who nonetheless draws the true map, because only the child's eye sees clearly |
| [[Anna]] | the present |
| [[Nan]] | the age she never reached, still living in 1998 |

**[[Elle]] is a fourth kind again** — not a life stage but a **faculty**: Anna's dream, personified as an insomniac who cannot sleep and therefore cannot dream. [[Mr. Vacancy]] has been in that dream long enough to be named from her mouth.

**None of it is ever confirmed.** A player who reads them as family members is not wrong in any way the game will contradict, and a player who reads them as states is not wrong either. Nothing names them, and there is deliberately **no document** — no deed, no contract, no register — that would settle it.

## Concealment method

Never stated, never confirmed, never confirmed-by-denial. Discoverable only by accumulation, across at least three independent channels so no single find gives it away:

- Missing-person paperwork and next-of-kin fields.
- Newspaper articles from 1998.
- Hidden voice messages and the corridor radio — [[Renner]] is every radio in the game, which is a father's voice under every door for seven days before the player knows whose it is.
- **Unremarkable patterns in how they describe her.** The likeliest and cheapest channel: two people who use the same specific phrase about the same girl, and neither of them ever says why.
- Fluorescent marks under UV, on the building and on objects — [[Filters]] does not read files.
