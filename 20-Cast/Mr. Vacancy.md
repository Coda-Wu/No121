# Mr. Vacancy

> [!note] 🔧 Name is a placeholder — the final name is Coda's development-time call (see [[Workshop Agendas]]). The character itself is ratified; only the name is open, which is why this note is `draft` and not `locked`.

## Role

The antagonist: the pollution that causes sick-weird anomalies and spatial rot. A building's natural predator — **emptiness**. [[The Sealed Fifth Floor]], the "no vacancies" policy, and the Administration's triage are all siege logistics against it.

> [!warning] Top-secret, revised in Round 6: **it existed before Anna died.** It is a demon. It cursed her family and took them one at a time — [[Pip]], [[Renner]], [[Eve]], and finally [[Anna]], who was the one it wanted. See [[The Curse]].
>
> Inside the vessel it is additionally fed by: **entropy**, **[[Anna]]'s dying wish** as she loses hope of being answered over five years, and **[[Otto]]'s guilt and heartbreak**. That is the layer that gives it his face. Outside, it is a predator with intent; inside, it grows on despair.

**Its method is information mismatch.** It opens a gap between what a person perceives and what is happening, and lives in the gap. It made the wind that blinded Otto on the beach, and it has been working the resulting mismatch on both of them ever since.

It is the source of **illusion horror and unknown horror**. The body-horror anomalies come from the decaying body instead — see [[The Two-Grammar Rule]] and [[The Body]].

## The consent rule

A death wish cannot act without being let in. Both [[Anna]] and [[Otto]] are still holding their last measure of will toward life, and while they hold it the Vacancy cannot take either of them.

Its plan is to **delude Otto into giving up first**. If he lets go, Anna is available immediately.

## Two tiers

- **The Vacancy itself never attacks and never threatens. It offers.** Illusion horror: it shows Otto what he wants — Anna, restored and correct in every detail — and offers her, early and for free. Every offer is a preview of [[Do I Know You — The Hollow Return]]: the Anna it renders does not recognise him. The horror is the fear that he will say yes.
- **The lesser shadows and hatched [[Festering]]s do attack**, and do try to drive him mad. They are the death wish's lower expressions, and their function is to exhaust him until an offer looks reasonable. This is why [[Purge and Light]] exists.

Design note: death is supposed to feel peaceful. It does not feel peaceful to a soul that is unwilling.

## The delusions

Each delusion is built from **something Otto fears or worries about**, and delivered through a gap he cannot close. The demon is death; it is not bound by the Building's rules and can approach from any direction.

**Settled:**
- **Demo, [[Mirror Maze]].** The three prints develop as: standing · rushing the camera · **a raven**. Otto then drops into a mind-space: a beach, the sound of ravens, and far off a group of them feeding on a body. The fear worked: that Anna is dead and her body was consumed.
- **D6, the phantom.** A delusion of Anna appears, convincing enough that the player believes the search is over. Something is wrong with her; **NIR** shows Otto she is not there. 🔧 The tell must be analog, not a digital glitch — see the banned list in [[The Two-Grammar Rule]].
- **D7, the offer of the post.** It asks Otto to hand back the camera, in the wording of the requisition slip: RETURN UPON TERMINATION OF TENANCY. If the player gives it up before the final maze, Otto has no weapon against the shadows and goes to [[Bad Ending — Ignorant Breach]] with no further choice.

🔧 The remaining delusions are Round 7 business.

## The consent rule

Deliberately **not** a palindrome — the inhuman thing doesn't get a loop-name, because under the secret layer of [[Naming Canon]] a palindrome marks someone Anna knew in life, and it knew nobody. A failed or near-palindrome is its typographic signature; [[Room 696]]'s doorsign reads 666 where the middle 6's nail has fallen.

**Imperfect mimicry, re-read:** it copies lawful patterns and gets exactly one detail wrong because **it is trying to be the thing that used to run this place, and it cannot manage the details**. 696 wanting a palindrome mask is a character beat — it wants a loop-name and cannot have one.

**Endgame role — THE USHER.** It wants the Building empty, and an ignorant Breach does its work. On the final night the doppelganger *encourages* opening the door.
