# Elle

## Role

The Insomniac of [[Room 232]]. She watches the corridor all night and has never once seen it change.

> [!warning] Top-secret: **Elle is [[Anna]]'s dream.** Not a person Anna knew and not one of her life stages — a faculty of hers, personified. See [[The Hidden Family]].
>
> That is why she cannot sleep, and why she cannot dream. [[Mr. Vacancy]] has crept into that dream for so long that **she is the one who knows its name** — which is how the antagonist gets named in [[03 LIVED — The Name]]. Curing her insomnia is restoring Anna's capacity to dream.

Renamed from Bob in Round 11: a male figure standing for a woman's dream was the wrong shape. *Elle* keeps the four-letter palindrome and means *she* in French, which is never pointed at.
