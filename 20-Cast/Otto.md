# Otto

## Role

Protagonist. A handyman outside; the Building's designated handyman inside — **HANDYMAN (ACTING)**. He returns to the building where he grew up and is quietly conscripted as its caretaker. Dual persona under [[Persona Law]]: Agent Otto (suit) is the hub self, Handyman Otto (uniform) the floor self; the [[Elevator]] is the only trigger.

Lives in [[Room 202]], which was [[Renner]]'s. Reports nightly to [[Anna]] on tape.
